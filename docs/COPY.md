## `/legal` page outline

**Title:** Legal & privacy

1. **About FITLAP.** An independent project by Mar Wie Ang, not affiliated with any laptop brand or retailer.
2. **Prices.**
   - **New prices** are official suggested retail prices (SRP) in the Philippines. Grey-market and imported units are not included.
   - **Used prices** are estimates compiled from public sources and reviewed by hand.
   - Prices change, so check with the seller before buying. Each laptop shows when its price was last checked.
3. **Specs, scores and battery estimates.** They're compiled from manufacturer pages and published reviews with AI assistance, then reviewed by hand. Battery hours and build scores are estimates.
4. **Privacy.**
   - No accounts, no cookies and no tracking or analytics for visitors.
