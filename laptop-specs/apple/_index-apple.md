## Not covered / unverified
- Used PH prices: none found for any model. Carousell PH returned 403 and TipidPC search returned no listings. All `ph_price_used_php` are null.
- PH prices for older models are Apple PH launch SRPs from PH tech news (Spin.ph, Revu, Unbox, YugaTech, Gadget Pilipinas, NoypiGeeks). Many build-to-order config prices are Unverified.
- Current models (M5 Air, M5 Pro/Max, MacBook Neo, 14-inch M5) use Apple PH store "from" prices after the 25 June 2026 price increase, plus Power Mac Center listings. Power Mac Center product titles do not state RAM, so some config-to-price matches are marked as such.
- MacBook Pro 13-inch M2 and 14-inch M5 launched with a single-chip lineup; the 14-inch M3 and M4 files combine base, Pro and Max chips because Apple sold them as one model year and size.
- MacBook Neo: keyboard backlight and Bluetooth version not listed by Apple (Unverified).
- No MacBook launches found between April and early October 2026. Rumored late-2026 models (M6 MacBook Pro, OLED/touch MacBook) are not covered.
- Beyond the Box catalog JSON was rate-limited (HTTP 429), so only older Beyond the Box data points are used.
