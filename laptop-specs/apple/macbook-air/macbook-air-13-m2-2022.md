## Best for
- Day-to-day and school: silent fanless design, 15–18 h battery, light at 1.24 kg.
- Coding (light to moderate): fine for web and scripting. Get 16 GB if you run Docker or many browser tabs.
- Business travel: thin, MagSafe charging, long battery.
- Dealbreakers: 8 GB base RAM is soldered. Base 256 GB SSD is slower than the 512 GB model (single NAND chip). Only one external display natively. Fanless chip throttles under long rendering or gaming loads.
