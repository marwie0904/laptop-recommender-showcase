## Best for
- Day-to-day and school: silent, 15–18 h battery, 1.24 kg.
- Coding (light to moderate): pick 16 GB. 8 GB launch units are limiting for Docker or heavy IDEs.
- Business travel: thin and light, two external displays when docked with lid closed.
- Dealbreakers: 8 GB units (pre-Oct 2024 stock) are soldered at 8 GB. No HDMI or SD. Fanless throttling under sustained loads.
