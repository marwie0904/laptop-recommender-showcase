## Best for
- Day-to-day and school: silent, 15–18 h battery, 1.23 kg, 512 GB base storage.
- Coding: 10-core CPU and 16 GB base. Go 24 GB or more for containers or local AI models.
- Business travel: Wi-Fi 7, two external displays, 12 MP camera.
- Dealbreakers: price jumped about 23% after the June 2026 increase, so the M4 Air or MacBook Neo may be better value. No HDMI or SD. Fanless throttling under sustained loads.
