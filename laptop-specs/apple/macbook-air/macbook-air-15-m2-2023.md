## Best for
- Day-to-day and school: large screen, silent, 15–18 h battery.
- Coding and spreadsheets: more screen space than the 13-inch for split windows.
- Business travel: 1.51 kg and 11.5 mm thin for a 15-inch.
- Dealbreakers: 8 GB base RAM soldered. One external display only. No HDMI or SD slot. Fanless, so long heavy loads throttle.
