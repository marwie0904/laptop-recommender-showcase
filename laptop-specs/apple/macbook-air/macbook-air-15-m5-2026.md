## Best for
- Day-to-day and school: large screen, silent, long battery.
- Coding and office multitasking: 512 GB base, room for side-by-side windows.
- Business travel: Wi-Fi 7, 1.51 kg for a 15-inch.
- Dealbreakers: post-June 2026 price is close to a 14-inch MacBook Pro M5 on sale. No HDMI or SD. Fanless throttling under sustained loads.
