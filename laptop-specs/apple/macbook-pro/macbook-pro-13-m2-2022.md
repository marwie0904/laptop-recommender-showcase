# Apple MacBook Pro 13-inch (M2, 2022)

Last 13-inch MacBook Pro and last Mac with a Touch Bar. Same M2 chip as the 2022 Air but with a fan for sustained loads and a bigger battery, in the older 13.3-inch chassis.

## Best for
- Coding and sustained workloads: the fan lets M2 hold clocks longer than the fanless Air.
- Day-to-day and school: 17–20 h battery.
- Dealbreakers: 720p webcam, only two ports, dated thick bezels, Touch Bar instead of function keys, 8 GB base RAM soldered. The 2022 Air is lighter with a better screen and camera for most buyers.
