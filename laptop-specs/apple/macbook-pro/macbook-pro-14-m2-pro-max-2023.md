## Best for
- Content creation and rendering: M2 Max GPU and up to 96 GB memory handle 4K/8K video and 3D work. Mini-LED XDR suits HDR grading.
- Coding: 12-core CPU, 120 Hz screen, long battery for a pro machine.
- Business travel: 1.6 kg with HDMI and SD slot, no dongles needed.
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM and SSD, so buy the memory you need upfront. Heavier than an Air. Superseded by M3 Pro/Max in Nov 2023.
