## Best for
- Content creation and rendering: M3 Max 40-core GPU with up to 128 GB memory and hardware ray tracing.
- Coding: M3 Pro balances CPU cores and battery. 120 Hz XDR display.
- Business travel: 1.55 kg, HDMI and SD slot built in.
- Dealbreakers: base M3 ships with 8 GB RAM and only two Thunderbolt ports. M3 Pro has lower memory bandwidth (150 GB/s) than M2 Pro. Soldered RAM and SSD.
