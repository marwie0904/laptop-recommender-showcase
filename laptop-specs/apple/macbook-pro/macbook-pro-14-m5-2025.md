## Best for
- Content creation: XDR mini-LED screen, fan for sustained exports, HDMI and SD slot.
- Coding: 10-core CPU, 16–32 GB, up to 24 h video battery.
- Business travel and outdoor use: 1000-nit SDR brightness, 1.55 kg.
- Dealbreakers: max 32 GB RAM and Thunderbolt 4 only (no TB5). Older Wi-Fi 6E radio, while the 2026 Air M5 got Wi-Fi 7. Soldered RAM and SSD.
