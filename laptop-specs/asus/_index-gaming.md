## Not covered / unverified
- Gaming lines present in the folder only for years listed above. No existence check was run for other ROG/TUF models or years (for example ROG Ally handhelds are excluded as non-laptops).
- Release month is null for TUF F15 2023, F17 2022, F17 2023 and Dash F15 2022 (not verified).
- Used PH prices: TipidPC blocks automated requests, so the four files added last have no used data. Other files per their own Pricing section.
- PC Express (pcx.com.ph/products.json) has no TUF F15, F17 or Dash F15 listings, so PH new prices come from ASUS PH only (F15 2023 and Dash F15 2022). TUF F15 2022 shows one SKU only.
- TUF F17 2022 and 2023: no PH price found. Per-model CPU/GPU mapping not on the ASUS spec page. F17 2022 GPU VRAM sizes are standard RTX 30 values, not shown on the fetched page.
- TUF F15 2023: ASUS lists RTX 4050 at the same 140W as RTX 4060, which is unusual and not cross-checked.
- TUF Dash F15 2022: ASUS spec page per-model lists were inconsistent. CPU/GPU pairing follows the PH SKU list.
- Zenbook, Vivobook, ProArt and ExpertBook files are owned by another agent and are not listed here.
