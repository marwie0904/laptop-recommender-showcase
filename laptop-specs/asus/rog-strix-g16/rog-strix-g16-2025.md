# ASUS ROG Strix G16 (2025) G615 / G614P

2025 Strix G16 in two versions: the redesigned Intel G615 (Core Ultra 9 275HX or Core i7/i9 14th-gen HX) and the AMD G614P (Ryzen 9 8940HX) in the older chassis. RTX 5050 to RTX 5080 at up to 175W. Mainstream-to-high-end gaming laptop.

## Best for
- Gaming: RTX 5070 Ti at 140W or RTX 5080 at 175W, 240–300Hz QHD+ panels.
- Rendering: 24-core Core Ultra 9 275HX, RTX 5080 16 GB, upgradeable RAM.
- School and coding: i7-14650HX + RTX 5050/5060 SKUs at ₱119,995–₱134,995 with 1 TB SSD.
- Dealbreakers: 2.5–2.65 kg plus a 280–380W brick. Most PH SKUs ship single-channel 16 GB (1 stick). RTX 5060/5070 limited to 8 GB VRAM.
