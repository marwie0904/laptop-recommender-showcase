# ASUS Vivobook 15 (2023, X1504VA / M1502YA)

The 2023 Vivobook 15: a slimmer 17.9 mm X1504 chassis with Intel 13th-gen U-series, while the AMD side continued in the M1502 chassis with Ryzen 7030-series U chips. Budget 15.6-inch laptops for school and home.
