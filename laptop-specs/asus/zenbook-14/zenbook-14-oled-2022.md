# ASUS Zenbook 14 OLED (2022)

The 2022 redesign of ASUS's mainstream 14-inch ultrabook with a 2.8K 90Hz OLED, a 75 Wh battery and Intel 12th-gen P-series (UX3402ZA) or AMD Ryzen 5000 (UM3402YA) chips. It targets students and office users who want a premium screen at a mid-range price.
