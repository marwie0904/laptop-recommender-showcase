# ASUS Zenbook 14 OLED (2024)

Redesigned 1.2 kg Zenbook 14 with Intel Core Ultra (Meteor Lake, UX3405MA) or AMD Ryzen 8040HS (UM3406HA), a 75 Wh battery and FHD 60Hz or 3K 120Hz OLED. A mainstream ultrabook aimed at students and professionals who want long battery life and a good screen.

## Best for
- School and day-to-day: 1.2 kg, 75 Wh, roughly 19 h video playback reported for the FHD AMD model (Notebookcheck).
- Coding and office multitasking: H-series CPUs, 16 GB minimum.
- Light content creation: 3K 120Hz OLED with full DCI-P3.
- Dealbreakers: RAM soldered; integrated graphics only; AMD model lacks Thunderbolt.
