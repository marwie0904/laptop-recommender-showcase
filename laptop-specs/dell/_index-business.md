## Not covered / unverified

- Dell support page not found for the slugs tried (en-ph): Vostro 14 3445 (AMD twin of 3440), Vostro 15 3540 / 3550, Vostro 14 3450, Vostro 14 5330, Vostro 14 5440, Dell Pro 16 Premium (PA16250), Dell Pro Max 14 Plus (MB14250). These models may exist and are simply not covered.
- Not attempted: Vostro 7000 (outside the 3000/5000 scope), 2021-launch models (Latitude 3120/3190/3420/3520, 5x20/5x21, 7x20, 9420/9510, Precision 3560/3561/5560/5760/7560/7760, Vostro 3400/3410/3510), Latitude Rugged, Chromebooks, rugged tablets, 2026 refreshes other than Dell Pro 14 Premium PA14260.
- AMD Vostro 3535/3435/3525/3425 and Dell Pro PC16255 / PB16255 / PV15255 are listed inside their Intel twin's file, not as separate files.
- Latitude 5000 files (12) and Dell Pro 14 / Pro 14 Plus files were written by the earlier agent and were not re-verified or edited.
- Release month is `null` in every new file: Dell manuals do not give a launch date.
- Chassis material, fingerprint reader, speaker, keyboard and wireless details are `Unverified` where the Dell manual section did not list them. GPU TGP values are `Unverified` for all discrete GPUs (Dell manuals omit them).
- Memory speed (MT/s) is `Unverified` in nearly all files. Dell manuals state the memory type but not the speed.
- New PH prices exist only for Latitude 5450 / 5455 / 7455 (Villman), Dell Pro 14, Dell Pro 14 Plus, Dell Pro 14 Essential and Dell Pro 15 Essential. All other models show `No current PH new listing found`. PC Express was not queried successfully because its products.json returned a bot-check page after the first request.
- Used prices are thin. Most Latitude 7000/9000, Vostro, Dell Pro and Precision models have 0-5 Carousell PH listings. Files with a single listing state `No reliable PH used-market data found` and name the price. Files with 2-4 listings give a range and note the small sample. Shopee, Lazada and TipidPC were not searched.
- Latitude 7440 / 7450 / 7340 / 7350 files each cover Mainstream, Ultralight and 2-in-1 versions in one file. The used price range blends all three.
- Dell manual inconsistencies kept as published: Latitude 9330 and Vostro 5630 / 5320 core and thread counts, Precision 5470/5480/5490/5570 chassis heights (7-11 mm), Vostro 5630 "RTX 2050 Ti" label.
