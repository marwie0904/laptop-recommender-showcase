# Dell Alienware 18 Area-51 AA18250 (2025)

Successor to the m18: 18" desktop-replacement gaming laptop with Core Ultra HX CPUs and RTX 5060 to RTX 5090. Dell's manual also lists "Plus" HX CPUs under the same model number. For buyers who want a desktop-class machine and will rarely carry it.
