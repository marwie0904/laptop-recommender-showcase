# Dell Alienware 16 Aurora AC16250 (2025)

Entry Alienware gaming laptop that replaced the m16 line in 2025. Intel Core 5/7/9 H-series with RTX 5050 to RTX 5070 (the manual also lists RTX 3050 and 4050 for some regions). Targets mainstream gamers who want an Alienware at a lower price than the Area-51.

## Best for
- Gaming: RTX 5060 at ₱99,990 is the value pick; 16" 2560x1600 120 Hz panel.
