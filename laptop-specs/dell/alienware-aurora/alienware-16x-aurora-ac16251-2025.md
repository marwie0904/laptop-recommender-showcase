# Dell Alienware 16X Aurora AC16251 (2025)

Step-up version of the 16 Aurora using HX-class Core Ultra CPUs (up to Ultra 9 290HX Plus), RTX 5060 to 5070 Ti and an optional 240 Hz OLED. Targets gamers who want HX performance in a 2.66 kg, 19 mm body.
