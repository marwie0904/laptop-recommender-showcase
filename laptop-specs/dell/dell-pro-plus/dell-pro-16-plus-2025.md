# Dell Pro 16 Plus (2025)

Upper-mid tier 16-inch 16:10 business laptop in the 2025 Dell Pro lineup (successor to the Latitude 5000/7000 16-inch class). Two Thunderbolt 4 ports, RJ45, HDMI 2.1, microSD, two DDR5 SODIMM slots and an optional QHD+ 120 Hz panel. Intel model PB16250 uses SODIMM RAM. AMD model PB16255 uses soldered LPDDR5X.
