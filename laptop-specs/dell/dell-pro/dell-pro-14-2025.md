# Dell Pro 14 (2025)

## Best for
- Day-to-day office work and business travel: 1.35 kg, RJ45, HDMI 2.1 and USB-A on board.
- Coding: RAM upgradeable to 64 GB, unlike most 2025 thin laptops.
- Dealbreakers: avoid the 128 GB UFS (soldered) and 300-nit 45% NTSC configs; only one Thunderbolt port.
