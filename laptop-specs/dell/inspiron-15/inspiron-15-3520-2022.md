## Best for
- School and day-to-day: cheap, full-size keyboard, upgradeable RAM and SSD.
- Dealbreakers: dim 250-nit 45% NTSC panel, DDR4 capped at 16 GB per Dell, HDMI 1.4 only, heavy for a 15 W laptop. Not for gaming or color work.
