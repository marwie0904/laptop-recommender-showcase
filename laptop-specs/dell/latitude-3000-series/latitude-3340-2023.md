# Dell Latitude 3340 (2023)

Compact 13.3-inch entry-level business laptop on 12th/13th Gen Intel U-series, sold as a clamshell and as a touch 2-in-1 with Wacom pen support. RAM is soldered LPDDR5 and capped at 16 GB. Successor to the Latitude 3330.
