# Dell Latitude 3440 (2023)

Entry-level 14-inch corporate laptop with 12th/13th Gen Intel U-series CPUs, two DDR4 SODIMM slots and a flip-down RJ45 port. Aimed at fleet buyers who need serviceability and RAM expansion, not thin-and-light design.
