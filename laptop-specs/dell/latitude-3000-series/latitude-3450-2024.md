# Dell Latitude 3450 (2024)

## Best for
- Day-to-day office work and school: 1.50 kg, USB4, DDR5 RAM expandable to 64 GB.
- Light business use at low used prices.
- Dealbreakers: the SSD slot only fits M.2 2230 drives; base 220-nit TN panel; the 15 W U-series chips are weak for sustained heavy work.
