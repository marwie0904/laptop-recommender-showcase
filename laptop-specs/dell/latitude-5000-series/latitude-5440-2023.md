## Best for
- Day-to-day office work and business travel: 1.39 kg, Thunderbolt 4, RJ45 and HDMI cover most docks and meeting rooms.
- Coding: two SODIMM slots allow 32–64 GB later, which few 14-inch laptops now offer.
- Used-market value: i5/i7 13th Gen units sell for about ₱21,000–₱30,000 in PH.
- Dealbreakers: base panels are 250-nit 45% NTSC, so pick the 400-nit option. MX550 adds little for real GPU work.
