# Dell XPS 13 9340 (2024)

Meteor Lake XPS 13 that merged the XPS 13 Plus design (capacitive function row, invisible haptic touchpad, zero-lattice keyboard) into the main XPS 13 line. Targets travelers who want a small premium aluminum ultrabook. PH retailers often list it as "XPS 13 Plus 9340".

## Pricing (Philippines)

## Best for
- Business travel: 1.2 kg, small footprint, long battery life on FHD+ panel.
- Coding and day-to-day: 16-core Meteor Lake, 120 Hz screen, up to 64 GB RAM.
- Dealbreakers: only two USB-C ports and no headphone jack, capacitive function row is hard to use by feel, soldered RAM, 8 GB base config should be avoided. Integrated graphics only, not for gaming or GPU rendering.
