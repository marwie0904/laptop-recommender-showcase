## Best for
- Coding and day-to-day: big 16:10 screen at 1.65–1.74 kg, Wi-Fi 7, three Thunderbolt 4 ports.
- Content creation: 3.2K OLED with 100% DCI-P3, Arc B390 for light editing.
- Dealbreakers: no discrete GPU (for heavy rendering or gaming look at the Creator Edition or Alienware), soldered RAM, 70 Wh battery is smaller than the 9640's 99.5 Wh.
