# HP Dragonfly G4 (2023)

HP's flagship 13.5" 3:2 clamshell business ultrabook at 0.99 kg, with Raptor Lake U chips (vPro options), Thunderbolt 4, optional 5G and OLED or Sure View privacy panels. Successor to the Elite Dragonfly G3 clamshell. Sold in Slate Blue or Natural Silver.

## Best for
- Business travel: under 1 kg, 20+ h claimed on the 68 Wh battery, optional 5G and Sure View privacy panel.
- Video calls: 5 MP IR camera, B&O four-speaker system.
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM (max 32 GB), U-series chips only, only one USB-A and two Thunderbolt ports, 45 Wh battery option is modest.
