# HP Elite x360 830 G10 (2023)

13.3" 16:10 convertible (360-degree) business laptop with a touch-only WUXGA panel, optional Sure View Reflect privacy, 5G, and an optional rechargeable active pen. Raptor Lake U chips with soldered LPDDR5.
