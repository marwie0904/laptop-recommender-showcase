# HP Elite x360 830 G11 (2024)

13.3" 16:10 convertible business laptop with Intel Core Ultra U (Meteor Lake, with NPU), soldered LPDDR5x up to 32 GB, pen support and optional 5G. Targets mobile staff who want a 2-in-1 with Thunderbolt 4 and HDMI 2.1. Successor to the x360 830 G10.
