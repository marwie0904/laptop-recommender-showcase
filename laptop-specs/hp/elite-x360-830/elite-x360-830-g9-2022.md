# HP Elite x360 830 G9 (2022)

13.3" 16:10 convertible business laptop with 12th-gen Intel Core U (10-core), soldered DDR5 capped at 16 GB, pen support, NFC and 5G/LTE options. Targets mobile staff who want a note-taking 2-in-1 with Thunderbolt 4. Succeeded by the x360 830 G10 in 2023.
