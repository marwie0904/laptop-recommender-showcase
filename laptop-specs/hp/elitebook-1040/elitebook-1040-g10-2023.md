# HP EliteBook 1040 G10 (2023)

Premium 14" 16:10 EliteBook with a magnesium chassis and 13th-gen Intel Core U/P vPro chips. Notebookcheck rated it 88%, praising the build, keyboard and serviceability, but called the 400-nit panel weak for the price.

## Best for
- Business travel: stiff magnesium body, LTE/5G with eSIM, smart card reader.
- Day-to-day: excellent keyboard and large touchpad.
- Coding: RAM up to 64 GB.
- Dealbreakers: tested panel dimmer than rated with only 88% sRGB, slow response; heavy sustained-load throttling on the i5; no SD reader.
