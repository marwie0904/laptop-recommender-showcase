## Best for
- Day-to-day office work: built-in RJ45, HDMI and two USB-A, long battery life (G1q).
- Business travel: 5G option and low fan noise.
- Coding: G1a Ryzen AI 7 350 is the safer choice for x86 dev tools.
- Dealbreakers: G1q runs x86 apps under emulation (up to ~25% slower) and has weak graphics; touch only on 1200p panels.
