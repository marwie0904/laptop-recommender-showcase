# HP EliteBook 6 G1i 13 (2025)

13.3" 16:10 mainstream EliteBook with Intel Core Ultra 200U (Arrow Lake-U), two SODIMM slots, RJ-45 and two Thunderbolt 4 ports. A 14" version is covered in the EliteBook 6 G1i/G1a/G1q 14 file. Specs come from HP QuickSpecs (v15, 23 Sep 2026), cross-checked against the HP Japan CTO sheet and the Notebookcheck review unit. QuickSpecs lists worldwide options, so PH availability may be narrower.
