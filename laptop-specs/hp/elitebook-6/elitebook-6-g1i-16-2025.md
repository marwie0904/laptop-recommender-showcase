# HP EliteBook 6 G1i 16 (2025)

16" 16:10 mainstream EliteBook with Intel Core Ultra 200U/200H (Arrow Lake), two SODIMM slots, a numeric keypad, RJ-45 and two Thunderbolt 4 ports. Successor to the EliteBook 660 G11. Specs come from HP QuickSpecs (v16, 23 Sep 2026) and the HP Japan CTO sheet. QuickSpecs lists worldwide options, so PH availability may be narrower.
