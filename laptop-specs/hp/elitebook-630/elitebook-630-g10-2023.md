# HP EliteBook 630 G10 (2023)

Entry 13.3" EliteBook at 1.22 kg with Raptor Lake U chips, socketed DDR4 (up to 64 GB), RJ-45, HDMI 2.1 and one Thunderbolt 4 port. No AMD twin: Wikipedia lists no 635 G10 (HP sold a separate 635 Aero line instead, not covered here).

## Best for
- Light office use and travel: 1.22 kg, RJ-45 and HDMI 2.1 keep dongles away, RAM and SSD are upgradeable.
- Dealbreakers: 16:9 FHD at best, base panels are 250 nits 45% NTSC, only one Thunderbolt port, 42.75 Wh battery, DDR4.
