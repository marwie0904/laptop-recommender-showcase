# HP EliteBook 630 G11 (2024)

Entry 13.3" 16:10 EliteBook with Intel Core Ultra U (Meteor Lake), two Thunderbolt 4 ports, RJ-45 and socketed DDR5 up to 64 GB. HP offers plastic or metal lid/base builds. No AMD twin: Wikipedia lists no 635 G11 (HP sold a separate 635 Aero G11 instead, not covered here).
