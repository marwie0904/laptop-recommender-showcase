# HP EliteBook 630 G9 (2022)

Entry 13.3" 16:9 EliteBook at about 1.2 to 1.28 kg with 12th-gen Intel Core U chips, socketed DDR4 (up to 64 GB), RJ-45, HDMI 2.1 and one Thunderbolt 4 port. Predecessor of the 630 G10. Wikipedia also lists an AMD twin (635 G9), but no HP document or retail listing for it was found.

## Best for
- Light office use and travel: about 1.2 kg, RJ-45 and HDMI 2.1 keep dongles away, RAM and SSD are upgradeable.
- Dealbreakers: 16:9 FHD at best, base panels are 250 nits 45% NTSC, only one Thunderbolt port, 42.75 Wh battery, DDR4.
