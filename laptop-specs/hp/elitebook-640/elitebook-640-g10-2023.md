# HP EliteBook 640 G10 / 645 G10 (2023)

Entry EliteBook 14" with 13th-gen Intel Core U or P (640 G10) or AMD Ryzen (645 G10). Metal body, two SODIMM slots and an optional second M.2 2230 slot make it one of the most upgradeable 14" business laptops of its year.

## Best for
- Day-to-day office work: quiet fan, long battery, RJ45 and HDMI built in.
- Coding: RAM upgradeable to 64 GB and a second SSD slot (2230).
- School: durable metal body at a lower price than the 840.
- Dealbreakers: dim, low-gamut 1080p 16:9 panel; 720p webcam on most SKUs; chassis rigidity below class best.
