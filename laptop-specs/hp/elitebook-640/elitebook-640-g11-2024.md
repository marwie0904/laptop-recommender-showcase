# HP EliteBook 640 G11 / 645 G11 (2024)

Entry 14" 16:10 EliteBook with RJ-45, smartcard reader, two Thunderbolt 4 ports (Intel) or USB4 (AMD), and socketed DDR5 up to 64 GB. The 640 G11 uses Intel Core Ultra U and H (Meteor Lake). The 645 G11 is the AMD twin with Ryzen PRO 7035 U chips. HP offers plastic or metal builds.
