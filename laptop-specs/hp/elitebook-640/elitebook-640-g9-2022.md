# HP EliteBook 640 G9 / 645 G9 (2022)

Entry EliteBook 14" with a 16:9 panel, 12th-gen Intel Core U (640 G9) or AMD Ryzen 5000-series (645 G9). In 2022 it took over the role of the old ProBook 640 line. Cheaper than the 840 G9, with fewer premium features.

## Best for
- Day-to-day office work: RJ45, three USB-A ports and Thunderbolt mean no dongles.
- School / coding on a budget: 10-core i5-1235U is enough for IDEs and browsers.
- Dealbreakers: 16:9 1080p panel (not 16:10), 720p webcam, no 5G, DDR4, base 45% NTSC panel.
