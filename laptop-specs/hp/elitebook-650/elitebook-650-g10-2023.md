# HP EliteBook 650 G10 / 655 G10 (2023)

Entry EliteBook in 15.6" with a numeric keypad, RJ-45, Thunderbolt 4 and socketed DDR4 up to 64 GB. Sits between the ProBook 450 G10 and the EliteBook 860 G10. The 655 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7030 U (Barcelo-R), DDR4 and a 180-degree hinge, but no Thunderbolt.
