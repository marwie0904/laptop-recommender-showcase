# HP EliteBook 650 G9 / 655 G9 (2022)

15.6" entry EliteBook with RJ-45, numeric keypad and socketed DDR4 up to 64 GB. The 650 G9 uses 12th-gen Intel Core U. The 655 G9 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 5000 U (see Specs; HP QuickSpecs for the 655 G9 was not located, so the AMD side relies on an AMD partner data sheet). Optional GeForce MX570 on the Intel model.
