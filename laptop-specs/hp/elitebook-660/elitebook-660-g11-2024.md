# HP EliteBook 660 G11 / 665 G11 (2024)

16" 16:10 entry EliteBook with RJ-45, a smartcard reader and socketed DDR5 up to 64 GB. The 660 G11 uses Intel Core Ultra U or H with an optional RTX 2050. The 665 G11 is the AMD twin with Ryzen PRO 7035 U chips and USB4. Successor to the EliteBook 650 G10.
