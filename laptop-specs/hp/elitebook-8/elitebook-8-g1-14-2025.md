# HP EliteBook 8 G1i / G1a 14 (2025)

Successor to the EliteBook 840/845 under HP's 2025 naming. Aluminium 14" 16:10 with socketed RAM, Thunderbolt 4 and Wi-Fi 7. Notebookcheck rated the AMD G1a 87% and recommends it. The Intel G1i uses Core Ultra 200V (Lunar Lake) with soldered LPDDR5X per the HP Japan CTO sheet, so its RAM is not upgradeable.

## Best for
- Coding: up to 64 GB socketed DDR5, quiet and cool under load.
- Day-to-day: excellent keyboard, good speakers, smart card reader.
- Business travel: 62 Wh battery (~14 h cited), Wi-Fi 7, optional 5G.
- Dealbreakers: no OLED, no discrete GPU, cramped arrow keys.
