# HP EliteBook 8 G1a 16 (2025)

16" 16:10 premium-mainstream EliteBook with AMD Ryzen AI PRO 300 (Krackan Point), two SODIMM slots, Wi-Fi 7 and a 62 Wh or 77 Wh battery. The Intel sibling is the EliteBook 8 G1i 16 (separate file). No HP QuickSpecs or HP Japan sheet was found for the G1a, so specs come from the Notebookcheck review (77 Wh unit), retailer spec text and a parts catalog (ipc-computer). Option lists beyond what is shown are Unverified.
