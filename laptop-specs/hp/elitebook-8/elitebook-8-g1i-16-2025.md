# HP EliteBook 8 G1i 16 (2025)

Premium 16" 16:10 EliteBook with Intel Core Ultra 200V (Lunar Lake), onboard LPDDR5X, a 62 Wh battery and a numeric keypad. Specs come from the HP Japan CTO spec sheet (no HP QuickSpecs found). Japan CTO lists only options sold in Japan, so the PH option list may differ. Sibling of the EliteBook 8 G1a 16 (AMD) and EliteBook 8 G1i 14.
