# HP EliteBook 8 G2i 14 / G2a 14 (2026)

Premium-mainstream 14" 16:10 EliteBook with onboard LPDDR5x. The G2i uses Intel Core Ultra 300 (Core Ultra 7 355 on the PH listing, Panther Lake-H Core Ultra 5 338H / Ultra X7 358H on the HP Japan sheet). The G2a uses AMD Ryzen AI 400 in the same 315.6 x 222 mm chassis. Listed in PH from mid 2026 (G2i only). Specs come from the HP Japan CTO sheet, a Notebookcheck review of the G2a, and HP spec text on one PH listing (no HP QuickSpecs located). Battery size differs between sources (62 Wh HP Japan, 68 Wh PH listing and Notebookcheck G2a).
