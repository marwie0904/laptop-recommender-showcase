# HP EliteBook 830 G10 / 835 G10 (2023)

13.3" 16:10 corporate ultrabook from 1.27 kg with 13th-gen Intel Core U, soldered LPDDR5 up to 32 GB, Thunderbolt 4 and optional Sure View privacy. Successor to the 830 G9. The 835 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7040 U-series. Intel data is from the HP QuickSpecs and datasheet. 835 G10 data is from retailer listings and the Notebookcheck deal article because its HP QuickSpecs could not be retrieved.
