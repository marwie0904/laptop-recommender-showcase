# HP EliteBook 830 G11 / 835 G11 (2024)

13.3" 16:10 corporate ultrabook at 1.29 kg with Intel Core Ultra U (Meteor Lake), soldered LPDDR5x, Thunderbolt 4 and optional Sure View privacy panels. Successor to the 830 G10. The 835 G11 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 8040 U chips (up to 16 TOPS NPU) and the same 56 Wh battery.
