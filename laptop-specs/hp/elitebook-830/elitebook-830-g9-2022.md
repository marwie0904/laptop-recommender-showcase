# HP EliteBook 830 G9 / 835 G9 (2022)

13.3" 16:10 corporate ultrabook from 1.27 kg. The 830 G9 uses 12th-gen Intel Core U (10-core) with soldered DDR5 capped at 16 GB. The 835 G9 is the AMD twin with Ryzen PRO 6000 U and Radeon 680M. Intel option data is from the HP QuickSpecs. AMD data is from Notebookcheck because the 835 G9 QuickSpecs could not be retrieved.
