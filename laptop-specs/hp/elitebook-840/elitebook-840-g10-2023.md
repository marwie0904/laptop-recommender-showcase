# HP EliteBook 840 G10 / 845 G10 (2023)

14" 16:10 corporate laptop. The 840 G10 uses 13th-gen Intel Core (Raptor Lake U and P). The 845 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7040 U/HS chips. Same chassis as the 840 G9, with socketed DDR5 and Thunderbolt 4 on both.

## Best for
- Coding and office work: up to 64 GB socketed DDR5 and P-series (840) or HS-series (845) CPUs.
- Business travel: 1.36–1.40 kg, Thunderbolt 4, optional 5G/LTE and Sure View privacy panel.
- Day-to-day: two USB-A, two Thunderbolt and HDMI 2.1, so few dongles needed.
- Dealbreakers: base 250-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim, 38 Wh battery option is small.
