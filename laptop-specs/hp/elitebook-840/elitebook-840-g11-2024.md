# HP EliteBook 840 G11 / 845 G11 (2024)

Mainstream 14" 16:10 corporate laptop with Intel Core Ultra (Meteor Lake) U and H chips, two SODIMM slots and Thunderbolt 4. Targets office fleets that want upgradeable RAM and strong manageability. The 845 G11 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 8040 U/HS chips, DDR5-5600 SODIMMs (max 64 GB), a 56 Wh battery, 1.414 kg and a claimed 16 h 15 min.

## Best for
- Coding and office work: up to 64 GB socketed RAM and H-series options.
- Business travel: 56 Wh battery (HP rates up to ~15 h MobileMark 25), optional 5G and Sure View privacy panel.
- Day-to-day: two USB-A plus two Thunderbolt 4 and HDMI 2.1, so few dongles needed.
- Dealbreakers: base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; avoid it. Not light for a 14" (1.42 kg+).
