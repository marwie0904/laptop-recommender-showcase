# HP EliteBook 840 G9 / 845 G9 (2022)

14" 16:10 corporate laptop sold as the 840 G9 (Intel 12th-gen P-series) and 845 G9 (AMD Ryzen PRO 6000) in the same aluminium chassis. Aimed at office fleets that want socketed RAM, a serviceable design and strong security features. Intel option data is from the HP QuickSpecs. AMD (845 G9) data is from Notebookcheck because the 845 G9 QuickSpecs could not be retrieved.

## Best for
- Coding: 845 G9 Ryzen 9 PRO 6950HS beat Intel rivals in multi-core tests; socketed RAM to 64 GB.
- Day-to-day / business: good keyboard, 5 MP webcam, smart card and fingerprint security.
- Business travel: LTE/5G option and bright 1000-nit Sure View panel.
- Dealbreakers: slow panel response (ghosting), CPU throttles ~27% under sustained load (840 G9), heavy for a 14" at ~1.47 kg, no SD reader.
