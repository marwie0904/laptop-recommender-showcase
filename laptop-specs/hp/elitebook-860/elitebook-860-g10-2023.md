# HP EliteBook 860 G10 / 865 G10 (2023)

16" 16:10 mainstream corporate laptop with 13th-gen Intel Core U and P chips, Thunderbolt 4, up to 64 GB DDR5 and a 76 Wh battery option. Bigger-screen sibling of the EliteBook 840 G10. The 865 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7040 U/HS chips, DDR5-5600 SODIMMs and a 76 Wh battery option. Both are in the HP QuickSpecs.

## Best for
- Large-screen office work: 16" WUXGA, 76 Wh battery with up to 19.5 h claimed.
- Business travel and docking: Thunderbolt 4, optional 5G, Sure View privacy panel.
- Dealbreakers: 1.73 kg+, base 250-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim, WUXGA only (no QHD/OLED confirmed), no discrete GPU.
