# HP EliteBook 860 G11 / 865 G11 (2024)

16" 16:10 corporate laptop with Intel Core Ultra U and H chips (Meteor Lake), socketed DDR5-5600 up to 64 GB, Thunderbolt 4 with DP 2.1, a 76 Wh option and a 2.8K 120 Hz OLED option. The 865 G11 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 8040 HS chips and a 76 Wh battery option rated up to 20 h 15 min.
