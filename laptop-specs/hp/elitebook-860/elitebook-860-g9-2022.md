# HP EliteBook 860 G9 / 865 G9 (2022)

16" 16:10 corporate laptop. The 860 G9 uses 12th-gen Intel Core P and U with socketed DDR5 up to 64 GB. The 865 G9 is the AMD twin with Ryzen PRO 6000 (6850U / 6950HS class). Intel option data is from the HP QuickSpecs. AMD data is from Notebookcheck because the 865 G9 QuickSpecs could not be retrieved.
