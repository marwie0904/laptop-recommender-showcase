# HP EliteBook Ultra G1i 14 / Ultra G1q 14 (2024)

Premium thin 14" 16:10 business ultrabook with Copilot+ class silicon. The Ultra G1i uses Intel Core Ultra 200V (Lunar Lake) and the Ultra G1q uses Qualcomm Snapdragon X (Arm, Windows on Arm). Launch dates Unverified (Intel Lunar Lake shipped from Sept 2024, Snapdragon X from mid 2024). HP QuickSpecs were located for the G1q only (c08980879); the G1i is verified from the Notebookcheck review unit.
