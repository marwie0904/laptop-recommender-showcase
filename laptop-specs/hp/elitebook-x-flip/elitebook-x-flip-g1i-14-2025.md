# HP EliteBook X Flip G1i 14 (2025)

14" 16:10 convertible business laptop with Intel Core Ultra 200V (Lunar Lake), on-package LPDDR5x up to 32 GB, Wi-Fi 7, pen support and 5G/LTE options. Targets mobile staff who want a premium 2-in-1 with long battery life. The 2026 successor is the X Flip G2i 14 (Core Ultra 7 366H, OLED, noted in sources).
