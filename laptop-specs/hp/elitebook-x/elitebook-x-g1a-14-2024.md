# HP EliteBook X G1a 14 (2024)

HP's flagship AMD business 14" with Ryzen AI PRO 300 (Strix Point) and an optional 2.8K 120 Hz OLED. Fast for its size, but heavy and with soldered RAM. Different chassis from the Intel EliteBook X G1i (see that file).

## Best for
- Coding / heavy office work: 12-core Ryzen AI 9 HX at 50 W sustained in a 14".
- Business travel: 74.5 Wh battery with long runtime.
- Content creation: 2.8K 120 Hz OLED option.
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM, no WWAN, ~1.49 kg is heavy for its class, slow IPS response.
