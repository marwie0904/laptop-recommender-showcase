# HP 240 G10 / 245 G10 (2023)

Budget 14" business laptop. The 240 G10 uses 13th-gen Intel Core U/P and Alder Lake-N chips. The 245 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7000 U chips. Light (1.36 kg) with a 41 Wh battery, USB-C is data-only.
