# HP 240 G9 / 245 G9 (2022)

Budget 14" business laptop with 12th-gen Intel Core U/P, 1080p 16:9 panel and RJ45. Positioned for students and small offices. The 245 G9 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 5000 U (Ryzen 7 5825U, Ryzen 5 5625U, Ryzen 3 5425U per QuickSpecs), 41 Wh battery and a data-only USB-C port.

## Best for
- School: cheap, simple 14" for documents and browsing.
- Day-to-day office: RJ45 and HDMI built in.
- Dealbreakers: i3 with UHD graphics is slow for anything heavy, Wi-Fi 5 on common SKUs, small 41 Wh battery, 720p webcam, Pentium/Celeron options are very slow.
