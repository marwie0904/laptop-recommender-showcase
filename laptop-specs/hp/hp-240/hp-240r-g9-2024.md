# HP 240R G9 (2024)

Refresh of the 240 G9 chassis with 13th-gen Core U and newer Core 3/5/7 (Raptor Lake Refresh) chips, USB-C 10 Gbps with Power Delivery and DisplayPort, Wi-Fi 6, RJ-45, and a Copilot key. The "R" tier is the cut-price sibling of the 240 G10 and keeps the older G9 body (1.47 kg, 41 Wh). No AMD twin was found.
