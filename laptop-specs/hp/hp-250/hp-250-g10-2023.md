# HP 250 G10 / 255 G10 (2023)

Entry-level 15.6" business laptop. The 250 G10 uses 13th-gen Intel Core (Raptor Lake U/P) and low-end Intel N-series chips. The 255 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7000 U chips. Plain FHD/HD panels, USB-C is data-only, and charging is through a barrel-type AC port.

## Best for
- School and light office work: cheapest HP business tier, replaceable RAM and SSD.
- Day-to-day: 250 G10 with Core i5/i7 U is enough for Office, browser and video calls.
- Dealbreakers: 41 Wh battery, USB-C is data-only (needs the AC adapter), HDMI 1.4b, Wi-Fi 6 only, 45% NTSC base panel, no Thunderbolt.
