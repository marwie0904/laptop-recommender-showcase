# HP 250 G9 / 255 G9 (2022)

Entry-level 15.6" business laptop. The 250 G9 uses 12th-gen Intel Core U/P and low-end Pentium/Celeron chips. The 255 G9 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 5000 U chips. Has RJ-45 and an SD card reader, but a 41 Wh battery and data-only USB-C.

## Best for
- School and light office: SD card reader, RJ-45, 12th-gen Core i5/i7 U or Ryzen 5/7 are enough for Office and browsing.
- Dealbreakers: Wi-Fi 5 on common SKUs, 41 Wh battery, data-only USB-C, HDMI 1.4b, 45% NTSC base panel, Pentium/Celeron options are very slow.
