# HP 250R G10 / 255R G10 (2025)

Budget 15.6" business laptop on the 250 G10 body with USB-C 10 Gbps Power Delivery and a Copilot key, no RJ-45. The 250R G10 uses Core 3/5/7 chips. The 255R G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7000 U chips and DDR5.
