## Best for
- 1440p gaming: 9955HX3D with 3D V-Cache and a bright 240 Hz QHD+ panel.
- Coding and compiling: 16 Zen 5 cores.
- Dealbreakers: short battery life and high power draw, loud in Unleashed mode, no G-Sync, 3-zone lighting only.
