## Best for
- 1440p gaming: RTX 3070 Ti at 115 W with the QHD 165 Hz full-sRGB panel.
- Content creation: near-full sRGB panels and two M.2 slots.
- Dealbreakers: no MUX switch on the AMD model, 4-zone (not per-key) RGB, weak 720p webcam, power button beside Backspace.
