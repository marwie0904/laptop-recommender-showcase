# HP Omen Transcend 14 (2024)

HP's first 14" Omen: an aluminum ~1.64 kg gaming/creator laptop with Intel Core Ultra (Meteor Lake H), RTX 4050/4060/4070 and a 2.8K 120 Hz OLED on every SKU. Notebookcheck rated it 88.5% and called it a credible Razer Blade 14 alternative.

## Best for
- Content creation on the go: 100% DCI-P3 OLED and RTX 4060/4070 in a 1.64 kg body.
- Gaming while travelling: USB-C charging at 140 W.
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM, weak hinge and lid flex, no MUX/VRR, OLED PWM, GPU capped near 65 W.
