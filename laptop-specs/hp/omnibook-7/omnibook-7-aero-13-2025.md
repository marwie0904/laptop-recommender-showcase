## Best for
- Commuting students and business travel: ~1 kg with good battery despite 43 Wh.
- Day-to-day work: full-sRGB matte panel and full-size ports.
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM, no Wi-Fi 7 or Thunderbolt, weak iGPU, narrow clickpad.
