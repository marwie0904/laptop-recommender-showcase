# HP ProBook 4 G1a / G1q 14 (2025)

Successor to the ProBook 440/445 under HP's 2025 naming. Only the AMD G1a was verified. The Snapdragon G1q 14 specs come from HP QuickSpecs (c09102585, v6, Jan 2026). The Intel versions are in the ProBook 4 G1i 14 / G1iR 14 file. A no-frills office machine in reviews (ITPro 60%, TechRadar 80%).
