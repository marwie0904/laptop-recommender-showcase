# HP ProBook 4 G1i 16 / G1iR 16 (2025)

16" 16:10 successor to the ProBook 460 G11 under HP's 2025 naming, with Intel Core Ultra 200U/200H (Arrow Lake). The G1iR is a lower-priced refresh on Intel Core 5 120U silicon. Aimed at office and school users who want a bigger screen with a numeric keypad. Specs come from HP QuickSpecs (v11, 23 Apr 2026) and the Notebookcheck review. An AMD G1a 16 twin was not verified (the 2026 ProBook 4 G2a 16 is in the ProBook 4 G2 16 file).
