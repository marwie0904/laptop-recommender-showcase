# HP ProBook 4 G1i 14 / G1iR 14 (2025)

Mainstream 14" 16:10 business laptop, successor to the ProBook 440 G11. The G1i uses Intel Core Ultra 200U/200H (Arrow Lake). The G1iR is a refresh on older Intel Core 5 120U/220U silicon sold at lower prices. Two SODIMM slots, RJ-45 and 20 Gbps USB-C. The AMD/Snapdragon versions are in the ProBook 4 G1a/G1q 14 file.
