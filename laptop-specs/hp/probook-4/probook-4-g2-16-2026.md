# HP ProBook 4 G2i 16 / G2a 16 (2026)

Mainstream 16" 16:10 business laptop. The G2i uses Intel Core 5/7 (Series 3) with Wi-Fi 7. The G2a uses AMD Ryzen 5/7 with Wi-Fi 6E and USB-C at 10 Gbps. Successor to the ProBook 4 G1i 16. Specs come from the HP Japan CTO spec sheets (no HP QuickSpecs found). Japan CTO lists only options sold in Japan, so the PH option list may differ.
