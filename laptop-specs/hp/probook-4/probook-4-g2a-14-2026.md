# HP ProBook 4 G2a 14 / G2i 14 / G2ah 14 (2026)

Mainstream 14" 16:10 business laptop, successor to the ProBook 4 G1a/G1i. Three AMD and Intel flavors appear across sources. The Notebookcheck G2a unit has Ryzen AI 400, Wi-Fi 7 and a 68 Wh battery. The HP Japan G2a sheet lists Ryzen 5 230 / Ryzen 7 250, Wi-Fi 6E and a 56 Wh battery. The G2i (Intel) and G2ah (Ryzen 5 220) are PH-listed with a 56 Wh battery and aluminum chassis. Specs come from the HP Japan CTO sheets and Notebookcheck (no HP QuickSpecs found). Japan CTO lists only options sold in Japan, so the PH option list may differ.
