# HP ProBook 440 G10 / 445 G10 (2023)

Mainstream 14" 16:9 business laptop with 13th-gen Intel Core U and P chips, two SODIMM slots, RJ-45 and HDMI 2.1. Lighter than the 450 G10 and without a numpad. The 445 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7000 U chips (Ryzen 5 7530U / Ryzen 7 7730U, still Zen 3 Barcelo-U refresh). Its QuickSpecs was not located, so its detail comes from the Notebookcheck review of the Ryzen 5 7530U unit.

## Best for
- AMD value pick (445 G10): 1.32 kg, bright panel in review, but still DDR4, no USB4 and a 42 Wh battery.
- Day-to-day office and school: 1.38 kg, RJ-45, HDMI 2.1, upgradeable RAM and storage.
