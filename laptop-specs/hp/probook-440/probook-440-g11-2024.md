# HP ProBook 440 G11 / 445 G11 (2024)

Mainstream 14" 16:10 business laptop. The 440 G11 uses Intel Core Ultra U and H (Meteor Lake) with an optional RTX 2050. The 445 G11 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7035 U chips. Two SODIMM slots, RJ-45, USB-C at 20 Gbps (Intel) or 10 Gbps (AMD). Reviewed in PH by Manila Shaker (Oct 2024).
