# HP ProBook 440 G9 / 445 G9 (2022)

Mid-range 14" business laptop with 12th-gen Intel Core U, 16:9 1080p panel and two RAM slots. Aimed at SMB fleets and students who want a cheaper, upgradeable work machine. The 445 G9 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 5000 U (Barcelo-U) chips, DDR4-3200 up to 32 GB and NVMe up to 1 TB (Notebookcheck launch coverage, Jan 2022).
