# HP ProBook 450 G10 / 455 G10 (2023)

Mainstream 15.6" business laptop with a numeric keypad, RJ-45, two SODIMM slots and an optional second M.2 SSD. The 450 G10 uses 13th-gen Intel Core (U and P series) with an optional RTX 2050. The 455 G10 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7000 U chips.

- Office and school: numpad, RJ-45, HDMI 2.1 and two SODIMM slots at a business-tier price.
- Light creative work: the RTX 2050 option and QHD panel exist, but only on select SKUs.
