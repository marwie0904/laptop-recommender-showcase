# HP ProBook 450 G9 / 455 G9 (2022)

Mainstream 15.6" business laptop with 12th-gen Intel Core, numeric keypad, RJ-45, two SODIMM slots and an optional second M.2 SSD. The 455 G9 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 5000 U (Ryzen 7 5825U seen on Notebookcheck: 16 GB DDR4-3200, Radeon Vega 8, 1080p, 51.3 Wh, 1.74 kg, 4x USB-A, HDMI, RJ-45, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.2). Its QuickSpecs was not located.

## Best for
- Office and school: aluminum body, numpad, RJ-45 and socketed RAM at a business-tier price.
- Light GPU work: MX570 option is weak, only for video encode acceleration and light editing.
- Dealbreakers: 1.74 kg+, only one USB-C port, no Thunderbolt, base 45% NTSC panel, DDR4.
