# HP ProBook 460 G11 / 465 G11 (2024)

Mainstream 16" 16:10 business laptop with a numeric keypad. The 460 G11 uses Intel Core Ultra U and H (Meteor Lake) with an optional RTX 2050. The 465 G11 is the AMD twin with Ryzen 7035 U chips. Two SODIMM slots and RJ-45.
