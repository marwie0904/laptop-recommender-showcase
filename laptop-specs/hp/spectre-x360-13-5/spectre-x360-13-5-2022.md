# HP Spectre x360 13.5 (14-ef0 / 14t-ef000) (2022)

Premium 13.5" 3:2 convertible with 12th Gen Intel U-series CPUs, optional 3K2K OLED and pen support. Targets travelers and professionals who want a light, aluminum 2-in-1. HP later renamed this chassis "Spectre x360 14" (14-ef2, 2023).
