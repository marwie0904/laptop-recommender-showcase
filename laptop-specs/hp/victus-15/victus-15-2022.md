# HP Victus 15 (2022)

HP's entry gaming 15.6" laptop, refreshed in 2022 with Intel 12th Gen H (15-fa0) and AMD Ryzen 5000H (15-fb0) in a smaller chassis than the 2021 model. Targets first-time gamers on a tight budget.

## Best for
- 1080p esports and lighter AAA gaming: RTX 3050/3050 Ti at 75 W handles medium settings at 1080p.
- School and coding: 12th Gen H / Ryzen 5000H CPUs are fast for compiling and multitasking.
- Dealbreakers: 4 GB VRAM on every GPU option, HP-official 16 GB RAM ceiling, GTX 1650 SKUs get the small 52.5 Wh battery, no USB-C charging.
