# HP ZBook 8 G1i 16 (2025)

Entry 16" 16:10 mobile workstation under HP's 2025 naming (the line that replaced the ZBook Firefly 16), with Intel Core Ultra 200H or 200U chips and an optional NVIDIA RTX 500 Ada. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c09133729 (v8, Sept 2026). No 16" ZBook 8 G1a was found in HP documents (the G1a QuickSpecs covers 14" only).

## Best for
- Light CAD, coding and office work on a 16" screen with an optional RTX 500 Ada and Wi-Fi 7.
- Dealbreakers: 64 GB RAM ceiling, single SSD slot, RTX 500 Ada has only 4 GB VRAM, base panel is 62.5% sRGB.
