# HP ZBook 8 G2i 14 / G2a 14 (2026)

Second generation of HP's entry 14" ZBook 8, replacing the G1i/G1ak. G2i uses Intel Core Ultra series 3 (Panther Lake) with optional NVIDIA RTX PRO 500 Blackwell. G2a uses AMD Ryzen AI 400 PRO with Radeon graphics. HP says the Intel version supports up to 70 W max system TDP and the AMD version 35 W. Notebookcheck reports a US starting price of USD 2,199 for the 14 G2i. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c09233926 (G2i, v4, July 2026) and the G2i/G2a datasheets.
