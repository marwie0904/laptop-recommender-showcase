# HP ZBook Firefly 14 G10 / Firefly 14 G10 A (2023)

Thin 14" 16:10 mobile workstation built on the EliteBook 840 body. The Intel model (Firefly 14 G10) adds an optional NVIDIA RTX A500 4 GB. The AMD model (Firefly 14 G10 A) uses Ryzen PRO 7040HS with Radeon 780M/760M iGPU and DDR5-5600. DreamColor QHD+ panel option.

## Best for
- Light CAD, photo editing and Adobe work: RTX A500 plus DreamColor option, ISV-certified workstation line.
- Travel: 1.40–1.45 kg, USB-C charging, optional 5G.
- Dealbreakers: RTX A500 is entry-level (4 GB), single M.2 slot, 51 Wh battery only, WUXGA base panel is dim (250 nits, 45% NTSC).
