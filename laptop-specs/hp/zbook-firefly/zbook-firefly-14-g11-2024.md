# HP ZBook Firefly 14 G11 / Firefly 14 G11 A (2024)

14" 16:10 mobile workstation on the EliteBook 840 G11 body. The Intel model (Firefly 14 G11) uses Core Ultra U/H with an optional RTX A500 4 GB. The AMD model (Firefly 14 G11 A) uses Ryzen PRO 8040HS. WQXGA 120 Hz DreamColor option.
