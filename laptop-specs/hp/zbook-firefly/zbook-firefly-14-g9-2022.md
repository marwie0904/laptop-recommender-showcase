# HP ZBook Firefly 14 G9 (2022)

## Best for
- Light CAD, photo and Office work on the go: 1.44 kg, Thunderbolt 4, socketed RAM, optional DreamColor panel.
- Dealbreakers: the base config has no discrete GPU, T550 and A500 are entry GPUs (T550 capped at 20–30 W), single SSD slot.
