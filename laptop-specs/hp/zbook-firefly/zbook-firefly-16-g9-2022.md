# HP ZBook Firefly 16 G9 (2022)

16" 16:10 entry mobile workstation with 12th-gen Intel U and P chips and an optional NVIDIA T550. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c08143317 (v12, June 2024).

## Configurations
QuickSpecs lists options, not fixed SKUs. The first row is the Notebookcheck review unit.

| Intel Core i5-1235U to i7-1280P (QuickSpecs) | 10C (2P+8E) / 12T for U chips, 12C (4P+8E) / 16T and 14C (6P+8E) / 20T for P chips | Intel Iris Xe or NVIDIA T550 4 GB | 8–64 GB DDR5-4800 | 256 GB–2 TB SSD | 16" WUXGA | Unverified |
