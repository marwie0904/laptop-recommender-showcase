# HP ZBook Fury 16 G10 (2023)

HP's top 16" mobile workstation. 13th-gen Intel HX CPUs (up to Core i9-13950HX), NVIDIA RTX Ada or A1000 GPUs up to RTX 5000 Ada 16 GB, four SODIMM slots (128 GB) and a 95 Wh battery. DreamColor 4K and OLED panels are options.

## Best for
- Rendering and 3D/CAD: HX-class CPUs and up to RTX 5000 Ada 16 GB.
- Video and color work: 4K 120 Hz DreamColor or OLED panel options.
- AI/data work: 128 GB RAM ceiling and large local storage.
- Dealbreakers: 2.4 kg plus 230 W adapter, short real-world battery under GPU load, high price class.
