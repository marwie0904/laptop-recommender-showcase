# HP ZBook Fury G1i 16 (2025)

16" 16:10 high-end mobile workstation sharing the Fury G1i platform with the 18": Intel Core Ultra HX (Arrow Lake-HX), NVIDIA RTX PRO Blackwell graphics, four SODIMM slots, four M.2 slots, Thunderbolt 5 and a 99 Wh battery. Successor to the Fury 16 G11. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c09153575 (v15, Sept 2026). No Notebookcheck review of this model was found.
