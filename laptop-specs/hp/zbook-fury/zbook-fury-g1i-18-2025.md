# HP ZBook Fury G1i 18 (2025)

18" 16:10 flagship mobile workstation with Intel Core Ultra HX (Arrow Lake-HX), NVIDIA RTX PRO Blackwell graphics up to 24 GB, four SODIMM slots, four M.2 slots, Thunderbolt 5 and a 99 Wh battery. A 16" Fury G1i exists as a separate file. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c09153574 (v13, Sept 2026).
