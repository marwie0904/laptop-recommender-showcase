# HP ZBook Power 15 G10 / Power 15 G10 A (2023)

15.6" mobile workstation. The Power G10 uses 13th-gen Intel H chips with RTX A500/A1000 or RTX 2000/3000 Ada. The Power G10 A uses Ryzen 7040HS with RTX A1000, RTX 2000 Ada or GeForce RTX 4050. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c08620446 (Intel, v2, June 2023, retired mirror copy) and c08620692 (G10 A, v9, June 2024). HP did not make a 16" Power G10. The 16" Power starts at G11.
