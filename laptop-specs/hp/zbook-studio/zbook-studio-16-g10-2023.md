# HP ZBook Studio 16 G10 (2023)

Thin 16" 16:10 mobile workstation with 13th-gen Intel Core H and NVIDIA RTX Ada or GeForce RTX 40 GPUs (up to RTX 4080 12 GB). Options include DreamColor 4K 120 Hz and 4K OLED panels and a per-key RGB keyboard.

## Best for
- Rendering / CAD / ML: up to RTX 4000 Ada or RTX 4080 with i9-13900H.
