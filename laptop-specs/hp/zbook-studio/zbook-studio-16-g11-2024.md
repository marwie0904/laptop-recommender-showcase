# HP ZBook Studio 16 G11 (2024)

Thin 16" 16:10 creator workstation with Intel Core Ultra H (Meteor Lake), NVIDIA RTX Ada or GeForce RTX 4070 graphics, a 4K 120 Hz DreamColor option and an 86 Wh battery. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c08993016 (v7, Nov 2024).

## Configurations
QuickSpecs lists options, not fixed SKUs. The first row is the Notebookcheck review unit.

## Best for
- Video editing and 3D on the go: RTX 3000 Ada, 4K 120 Hz DreamColor panel, 1.74–1.8 kg, Wi-Fi 7.
- Dealbreakers: battery life is the weakest score in the Notebookcheck review (66%), webcam is only 720p, review unit USD 4,300, 64 GB RAM ceiling.
