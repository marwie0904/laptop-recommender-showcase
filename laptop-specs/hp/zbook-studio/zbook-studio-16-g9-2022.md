## Best for
- Rendering / CAD: RTX A2000 with certified pro drivers and 14-core i7.
- Content creation: 4K 120 Hz DreamColor panel with near-full DCI-P3.
- Coding: 64 GB RAM ceiling, quiet for its class.
- Dealbreakers: 720p webcam, low 50 W GPU power limit, keyboard regressions vs G8.
