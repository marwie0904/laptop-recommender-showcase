# HP ZBook Ultra G1a 14 (2025)

14" 16:10 compact workstation with AMD Ryzen AI Max PRO (Strix Halo), a large integrated Radeon GPU and up to 128 GB soldered LPDDR5x-8533 (up to 96 GB can be allocated to the GPU). Successor to the ZBook Firefly 14. Option lists come from HP QuickSpecs c09119722 (v10, Sept 2026).

## Configurations
QuickSpecs lists options, not fixed SKUs. The first two rows are the Notebookcheck review unit and a PH listing.
