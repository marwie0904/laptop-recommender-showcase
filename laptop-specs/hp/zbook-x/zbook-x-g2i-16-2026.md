# HP ZBook X G2i 16 (2026)

16" 16:10 workstation succeeding the ZBook X G1i, with Intel Core Ultra series 3 (Panther Lake) H chips, NVIDIA RTX PRO 500 to 3000 Blackwell graphics, up to 128 GB DDR5-6400 and a 96 Wh battery option.
