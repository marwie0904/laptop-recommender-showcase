## Unverified / known gaps

- `release_date` is null in every file: PSREF spec sheets carry no announce date and it was not verified elsewhere.
- New PH prices exist only for models currently on the Lenovo PH store or PC Express (mostly 2024–2026 models). Older models show `ph_price_new_php: null`. Villman, Octagon, LazMall and Shopee Mall were not checked (no machine-readable access); Carousell PH blocks automated access.
- Used prices come from a small TipidPC sample; most files have `ph_price_used_php: null`.
- ThinkBook 14 G7 ARP and ThinkBook 16 G7 ARP: PSREF lists only the processor family; CPU rows come from PH retail SKUs. ThinkBook 16 G7+ ASP CPU SKUs are Unverified (Ryzen AI 9 300 series per PSREF).
- 2026 CPUs (Intel Core Ultra series 3 'Panther Lake', Core series 3 'Wildcat Lake', Ryzen AI 400, Ryzen 100/200 refreshes): core/thread counts taken from PSREF processor tables, not cross-checked elsewhere.
- Display options are summarized per model (sizes, resolutions, panel types, refresh, brightness, gamut) rather than per panel SKU.
- iGPU names in configuration tables use a majority vote across PSREF processor tables; where not parsed the cell shows 'Integrated'.
