# Lenovo IdeaPad 5 2-in-1 Gen 10 (2025)

Mid-priced touch convertible with an aluminium lid, covering the PSREF machines IdeaPad 5 2-in-1 14IAL10 and 16IAL10 (Intel Core Ultra 200H/U Arrow Lake) and 14AKP10 and 16AKP10 (Ryzen AI 300 Krackan Point). PH retailers list the Intel 14" as "IdeaPad Slim 5i 2-in-1". Targets students who want a pen-capable convertible with OLED and Copilot+ options.
