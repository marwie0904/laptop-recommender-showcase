# Lenovo IdeaPad 5 2-in-1 Gen 9 (2024)

Mid-priced touch convertible that replaced the IdeaPad Flex 5 line, covering the PSREF machines IdeaPad 5 2-in-1 14AHP9 and 16AHP9 (Ryzen 8000 Hawk Point). Targets students who want a pen-capable 2-in-1 with an optional OLED panel. Intel variants of this generation (14IML9 / 16IML9) were not retrievable from PSREF, so only the AMD models are covered here.
