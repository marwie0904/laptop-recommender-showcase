# Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5 Gen 7 (2022)

360-degree convertible touchscreen laptop in 14" and 16" 16:10 sizes. PSREF machine names covered: 14IAU7, 16IAU7 (Intel 12th Gen U) and 14ALC7, 16ALC7 (AMD Ryzen 5000U). Targets students and note-takers who want a pen-capable 2-in-1 at a mainstream price.

## Best for
- Students and note-taking: touchscreen, 360-degree hinge and Lenovo Digital Pen support.
- Day-to-day office work on the 14" (about 1.5 kg).
- Dealbreakers: RAM soldered, so avoid 4 and 8 GB. Charges from a round-tip adapter. The 16" weighs 2.1 kg.
