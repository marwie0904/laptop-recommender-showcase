# Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5 Gen 8 (2023)

360-degree convertible touchscreen laptop. PSREF machine names covered: 14IRU8, 16IRU8 (Intel 13th Gen U), 14ABR8 and 16ABR8 (AMD Ryzen 5000 / 7030U). Targets students and note-takers who want a mainstream 2-in-1. A refresh of the Gen 7 design with a 13th Gen CPU and a 14" WUXGA OLED option.

## Best for
- Students and note-taking: touchscreen, 360-degree hinge, pen support.
- Day-to-day office work on the 14" (about 1.5 kg).
- Dealbreakers: RAM soldered, avoid 4 and 8 GB. Charges from a round-tip adapter. 60 Hz screens only. 16ABR8 weighs 2.1 kg.
