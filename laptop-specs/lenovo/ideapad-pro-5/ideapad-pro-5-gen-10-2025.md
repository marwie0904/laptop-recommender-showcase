# Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 Gen 10 (2025)

All-aluminium 14" and 16" 16:10 laptop with a 2.8K 120 Hz OLED as the only screen, 84 Wh battery, and an optional GeForce RTX 5050 on the 16". PSREF machine names covered: 14AKP10, 16AKP10 (AMD Ryzen AI 300) and 14IAH10, 16IAH10 (Intel Core Ultra 200H). Targets creators and students who want a bright OLED and an NPU in a thin body.
