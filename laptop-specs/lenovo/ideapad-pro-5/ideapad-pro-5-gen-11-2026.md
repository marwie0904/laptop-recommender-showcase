# Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 Gen 11 (2026)

All-aluminium 14" and 16" 16:10 laptop with a 2.8K 120 Hz OLED as the only screen, 84 to 99.9 Wh batteries, and optional GeForce RTX 5050 / 5060 on the 16IPH11. PSREF machine names covered: 14IPH11, 16IPH11 (Intel Core Ultra Series 3, Panther Lake) and 14AGP11, 16AGP11 (AMD Ryzen AI 400). PSREF sheets are dated May to September 2026. No Gen 11 Pro 5 listing was found at PH retailers checked in Oct 2026, so PH availability is not confirmed.

## Best for
- Creators: 100% DCI-P3 120 Hz OLED, 85 to 100 Wh battery.
- Light gaming and GPU editing: 16IPH11 with RTX 5050 / 5060 at 105 W, or Arc B390 integrated graphics on X-series chips.
