# Lenovo IdeaPad 5 Pro Gen 7 (2022)

All-aluminium 14" and 16" laptops that preceded the IdeaPad Pro 5 name, covering the PSREF machines IdeaPad 5 Pro 14IAP7, 14ARH7 (14", ultraportable Intel P / Ryzen 6000 HS) and 16IAH7, 16ARH7 (16", 2.5K display with optional GeForce or Arc GPU). Sold in PH as "IdeaPad Slim 5 Pro". Targets students and light creators who want a metal body with a 2.2K to 2.5K screen.
