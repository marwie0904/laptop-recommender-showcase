# Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 Gen 8 (2023)

All-aluminium 14" and 16" 16:10 laptop with high-refresh 2.5K / 2.8K screens and optional GeForce RTX 3050 / 4050 graphics on the Intel models. PSREF machine names covered: 14IRH8, 16IRH8 (Intel 13th Gen H) and 14ARP8, 16ARP8 (AMD Ryzen 7035HS). Targets students and creators who want a sharper, faster-refresh screen and GPU headroom in a slim body.

## Best for
- Creators and students: 100% sRGB high-res screens, up to 120 Hz.
- Light gaming and GPU-assisted editing: RTX 3050 / 4050 options on the Intel models (80 W on the 16").
- Dealbreakers: RAM is soldered, so buy 16 GB or more (14ARP8 tops out at 16 GB). The 14" RTX 3050 runs at only 60 W.
