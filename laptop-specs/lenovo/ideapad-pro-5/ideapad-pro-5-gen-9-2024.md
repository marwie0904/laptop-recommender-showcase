# Lenovo IdeaPad Pro 5 Gen 9 (2024)

All-aluminium 14" and 16" 16:10 laptop with 120 Hz 2.8K / 2.5K / 2K screens, 84 Wh battery and optional GeForce RTX 3050 / 4050 graphics. PSREF machine names covered: 14IMH9, 16IMH9 (Intel Core Ultra H) and 14AHP9, 16AHP9 (AMD Ryzen 8000 HS). Targets creators and students who want a bright high-refresh screen and a GPU in a 1.5 to 1.9 kg body.

## Best for
- Creators: 100% DCI-P3 OLED or 100% sRGB 120 Hz screens, 84 Wh battery.
- Light gaming and GPU editing: RTX 3050 / 4050 on 16" (80 W). 14AHP9 RTX 3050 runs at 60 W.
- Dealbreakers: RAM soldered, buy 16 GB or more. 14AHP9 has only one SSD slot.
