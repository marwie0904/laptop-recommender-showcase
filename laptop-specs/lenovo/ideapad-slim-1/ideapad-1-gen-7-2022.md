# Lenovo IdeaPad 1 Gen 7 (2022), sold in PH retail as "IdeaPad Slim 1" for some AMD listings

Lenovo's cheapest plastic laptop, named "IdeaPad 1" in PSREF (the "Slim 1" label appears only in some PH store listings). Filed as the Gen 7 entry of the Slim 1 family. Covers the PSREF machines IdeaPad 1 14IAU7, 15IAU7 (Intel 12th-gen), 15AMN7 (Ryzen 7000 U, PSREF page dated Jun 2026) and 15ALC7 (Ryzen 5000 U). Targets basic school and home use on the lowest budget.
