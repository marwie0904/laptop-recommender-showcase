# Lenovo IdeaPad 3 Gen 7 (2022)

Predecessor of the IdeaPad Slim 3 line (marketed as "IdeaPad 3" / "IdeaPad 3i" in 2022, not "Slim 3"), filed here as the Gen 7 entry of the Slim 3 family. Covers the PSREF machines IdeaPad 3 14IAU7, 15IAU7, 17IAU7 (Intel 12th-gen U-series) and 15ABA7 (AMD Ryzen 5000 U-series). Targets students and basic home use, and is notable for having a SODIMM slot and optional 2.5" HDD.

## Best for
- School or office use on a tight budget. The 17.3" variant gives a large screen at a low price.
- Dealbreakers: weak battery on 38 Wh models (7 hr video playback), 4-8 GB base RAM, TN panels, heavy and thick (19.9 mm).
