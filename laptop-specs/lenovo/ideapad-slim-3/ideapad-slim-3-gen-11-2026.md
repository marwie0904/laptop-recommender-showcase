# Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 3 Gen 11 (2026)

Thin-and-light with Intel Core Series 3 chips: Core Ultra 5 / 7 in the IPH11 models (Copilot+ PC class NPU) and Core 3 / 5 / 7 in the IWC11 models. Covers the PSREF machines IdeaPad Slim 3 14IPH11, 15IPH11, 14IWC11 and 15IWC11. Targets students and home or office users who want a larger laptop with an optional OLED panel and user-replaceable SODIMM and SSDs.

## Best for
- School and office: 6-8 core Series 3 chips handle browsing, documents and video calls.
- Copilot+ features: only IPH11 models have the 40+ TOPS NPU.
- Display upgrade: OLED options (14" 60 Hz, 15.3" 2560x1600 165 Hz) are rare at this tier.
- Dealbreakers: integrated graphics only. Some PH configs ship 8 GB RAM, though the SODIMM slot allows an upgrade.
