# Lenovo IdeaPad 5 Gen 7 (2022)

Predecessor of the IdeaPad Slim 5 line (named plain "IdeaPad 5" in 2022). 14" and 15.6" models with a 16:9 FHD screen, in Intel 12th Gen (14IAL7, 15IAL7) and AMD Ryzen 5000 (14ABA7, 15ABA7) versions. Targets students and general home or office use. Now an older platform with soldered DDR4.

## Best for
- School and basic office work: metal-lid body and 8 to 16 GB RAM.
- Light gaming or photo editing on 14IAL7 / 15IAL7 with GeForce MX550.
- Dealbreakers: 16:9 screens with a 45% NTSC option are dim and washed out, so pick the 100% sRGB IPS. RAM is soldered, so avoid 8 GB.
