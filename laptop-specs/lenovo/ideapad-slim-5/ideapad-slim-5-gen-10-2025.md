# Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 Gen 10 (2025)

All-aluminium 14" and 16" 16:10 laptop. Unlike Gen 8 and Gen 9, Gen 10 uses two DDR5 SODIMM slots and two M.2 slots. PSREF machine names covered: 14AKP10 and 16AKP10 (AMD Ryzen AI 300), 14IAH10 and 16IAH10 (Intel Core Ultra 200H), 14IRH10 and 16IRH10 (Intel Core i5-13420H / i7-13620H), 14IRH10R (Intel Core 5 / 7 Series 2 H). Targets students and office workers who want a metal body and room to upgrade.

## Best for
- Students and office: aluminium body, 60 to 80 Wh battery, user-upgradeable RAM and two SSD slots.
- Light creative work: 14/16" OLED options (100% DCI-P3), 16" 2.8K OLED at 120 Hz.
- Dealbreakers: 14IRH10 / 16IRH10 still use Raptor Lake 13420H / 13620H with UHD graphics, so the AKP10 or IAH10 gives better integrated graphics. Ryzen AI 5 330 models run the second SSD slot at PCIe x2.
