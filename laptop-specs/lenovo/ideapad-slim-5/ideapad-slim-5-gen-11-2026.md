# Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 Gen 11 (2026)

All-aluminium 14" and 16" 16:10 laptop with two DDR5 SODIMM slots and two M.2 slots. PSREF machine names covered: 14IPH11, 16IPH11 (Intel Core Ultra Series 3, Panther Lake) and 14AGP11, 16AGP11 (AMD Ryzen AI 400). PSREF sheets are dated September 2026. No Gen 11 Slim 5 listing was found at PH retailers checked in Oct 2026, so PH availability is not confirmed. Targets students and office workers who want a metal body and upgradeable RAM and storage.
