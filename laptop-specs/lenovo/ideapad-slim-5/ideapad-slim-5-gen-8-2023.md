# Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 Gen 8 (2023)

Thin-and-light aluminium-lid mainstream laptop in 14" and 16" 16:10 sizes, covering PSREF machine names 14IRL8, 16IRL8 (Intel 13th Gen), 14IAH8, 16IAH8 (Intel 12th Gen i5-12450H) and 14ABR8, 16ABR8 (AMD Ryzen 7030U). Targets students and office users who want a metal body and an optional OLED or 2.5K screen without a discrete GPU.

## Best for
- School and office work: metal lid, 16:10 screen, PSREF MobileMark 2018 ratings of about 9 to 15.5 hr depending on config.
- Light content creation: 14" OLED (100% DCI-P3) or 16" 2.5K panel options.
- Dealbreakers: RAM is soldered, so avoid 8 GB single-channel units (also drops Intel graphics to UHD-class performance). Screens are 60 Hz only.
