# Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 Gen 9 (2024)

Aluminium-lid 16:10 mainstream laptop with an NPU on every variant. PSREF machine names covered: 14IMH9 and 16IMH9 (Intel Core Ultra H), 14AHP9 and 16AHP9 (AMD Ryzen 8000 HS), 15IRH9 (15.3", Intel 13th Gen H) and 14Q8X9 (Snapdragon X, Copilot+ PC). Targets students and office users who want more CPU power than the Slim 3 plus an OLED option.

## Best for
- School and office: all-aluminium body, 16:10 screen, rapid charge.
- Light creative work and video calls: OLED options (100% DCI-P3), 16" 2K OLED at 120 Hz.
- Battery life and quiet use: 14Q8X9 (Snapdragon X, 57 Wh), with the caveat that some x86 apps run under emulation.
- Dealbreakers: RAM and wireless are soldered, so buy 16 GB or more. 15IRH9 8 GB is single-channel. Most 14" and 16" variants have a single-slot SSD (16IMH9 has two).
