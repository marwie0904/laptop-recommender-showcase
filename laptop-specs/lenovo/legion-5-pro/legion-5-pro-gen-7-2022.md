## Best for
- Gaming: RTX 3070 Ti at 150W with a 240Hz WQXGA panel.
- Content creation: factory-calibrated 100% sRGB, 500-nit WQXGA panel.
- Rendering: full-power GPU and 14-core i9/i7 H CPUs.
- Dealbreakers: 2.4-2.5 kg plus a large 300W brick, 720p webcam, RTX 3050 Ti (non-H) configs are weak for the price class.
