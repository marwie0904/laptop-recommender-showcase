# Lenovo Legion 5 / 5i Gen 7 (2022)

15.6" 16:9 gaming laptop in four PSREF variants: Legion 5i 15IAH7 and 15IAH7H (Intel 12th Gen H) and Legion 5 15ARH7 and 15ARH7H (AMD Ryzen 6000H). The non-H models carry RTX 3050 / 3050 Ti, the H models carry RTX 3060 to 3070 Ti. Targets mainstream gamers who want 144-165 Hz screens without the Pro-line 16" panel.

## Best for
- 1080p to 1440p gaming: the 140W H models with RTX 3060 to 3070 Ti drive 165 Hz panels.
- Upgrade-friendly buyers: two SODIMM and two M.2 slots.
- Dealbreakers: non-H models are capped at RTX 3050 / 3050 Ti 4GB and 16 GB RAM. 16:9 panels are 300 nits only. 2022 Wi-Fi 6/6E, no card reader.
