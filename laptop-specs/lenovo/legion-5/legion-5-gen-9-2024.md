# Lenovo Legion 5i Gen 9 (2024)

No AMD Gen 9 Legion 5 PSREF sheet was found (Legion_5_16AHP9 and Legion_5_15AHP9 returned 404), so AMD variants are not covered. ## Best for
- 1440p-class gaming: the 16" 16:10 WQXGA 240 Hz option with RTX 4060 / 4070 is the strong config.
- Upgrade-friendly buyers: 64 GB SODIMM max, two M.2 slots, microSD reader.
- Dealbreakers: the 15IRX9 is a single fixed config with a 60 Wh battery and 144 Hz 300-nit FHD panel. RTX 4050 has only 6 GB VRAM.
