## Best for
- Gaming: RTX 5070 Ti at 140W (16IAX10H) or RTX 5070 at 115W with a 240Hz OLED.
- Content creation: DCI-P3 OLED with HDR True Black 1000.
- Rendering: 16-core Ryzen 9 9955HX or 24-core Core Ultra 9.
- Dealbreakers: RTX 5060/5070 limited to 115W in the non-H model, OLED burn-in risk for static HUDs, 2.3-2.4 kg.
