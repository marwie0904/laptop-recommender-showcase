## Best for
- **Coding**: up to 32 GB RAM (SO-DIMM, upgradeable); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Ethernet for office docking and peripherals.
- **School**: entry business pricing.
- **Dealbreakers**: the base 250-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
