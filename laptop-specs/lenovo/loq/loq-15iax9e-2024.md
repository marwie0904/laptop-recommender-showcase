## Best for
- Budget gaming: entry RTX GPUs at 1080p medium settings, lighter (1.77 kg) than the standard LOQ.
- School: portable enough to carry daily.
- Dealbreakers: single RAM slot means single-channel memory, 65W GPU cap limits RTX 4050 performance, no USB-C charging or DisplayPort out.
