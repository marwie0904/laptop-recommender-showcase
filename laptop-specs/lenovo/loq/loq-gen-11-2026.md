## Best for
- Gaming: 115W RTX 5050/5060/5070 in every config, a step up from the 100W Gen 10.
- Content creation: 100% sRGB panels, WQXGA 400-nit option, Thunderbolt 4 / USB4 for fast external storage.
- School / coding: lighter (2.1 kg) and smaller than earlier LOQs, with long rated video playback (up to 15 hr on 15IPH11 per PSREF).
- Dealbreakers: Core Ultra 7 355 has only 8 threads. No card reader.
