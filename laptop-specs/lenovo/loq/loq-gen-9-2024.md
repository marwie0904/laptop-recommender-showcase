# Lenovo LOQ Gen 9 (2024)

Second-generation 15.6" LOQ entry gaming laptop. Covers PSREF machines LOQ 15IRX9 (Intel 13th/14th Gen HX), LOQ 15IAX9 (Intel 12th Gen HX, NVIDIA), LOQ 15IAX9I (Intel 12th Gen HX with Intel Arc A530M), LOQ 15AHP9 (AMD Ryzen 8040 / 100 series) and LOQ 15ARP9 (AMD Ryzen 7035HS), all in the same 2.38 kg chassis. The thinner LOQ 15IAX9E has its own file (loq-15iax9e-2024.md).

## Pricing (Philippines)
- New: ₱41,500 – ₱97,995 (PC Express, DynaQuest, EasyPC, checked Oct 2026). The EasyPC 83FQ000BPH listing is matched to 15IAX9I because the Arc A530M GPU appears only on that model. EasyPC also lists an unnamed LOQ with i5-12450HX / 12 GB / RTX 2050 at ₱43,125; that CPU/GPU pair matches both 15IAX9 and 15IAX9E, so it is not counted here.
- Used: No reliable PH used-market data found

## Best for
- Gaming: RTX 4050/4060 at 105-115W runs current games at 1080p high. The RTX 4070 (15ARP9) is the strongest option.
- School / coding: HX CPUs (15IRX9) handle compiling and multitasking well, and RAM and SSD are user-upgradeable.
- Content creation: 100% sRGB panels on every config.
- Dealbreakers: Arc A530M (15IAX9I) is well below RTX 4050 in games and driver support. 8 GB single-stick configs (83DV01F2PH) run single-channel. Ryzen 5 7235HS is only 4 cores. Battery life is short (60Wh, power-hungry HX CPUs).
