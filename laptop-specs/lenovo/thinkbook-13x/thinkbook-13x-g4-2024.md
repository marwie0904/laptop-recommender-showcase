# Lenovo ThinkBook 13x G4 (2024)

The ThinkBook 13x G4 is Lenovo's 2024 premium 13.3-inch small-business ultrabook, built on Intel H Series Core Ultra 5 / 9 Processor (Series 1). It targets professionals who want a very thin premium ThinkBook.
