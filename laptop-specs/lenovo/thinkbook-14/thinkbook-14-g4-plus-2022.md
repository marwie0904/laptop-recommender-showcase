## Best for
- **Rendering / 3D**: discrete GPU options (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2050 Laptop GPU 4GB GDDR6) plus up to 32 GB RAM.
- **Coding**: up to 32 GB RAM (soldered, choose capacity at purchase); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4, Ethernet for office docking and peripherals.
- **School**: entry business pricing.
- **Dealbreakers**: RAM is soldered; buy the capacity you need upfront; the base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available.
