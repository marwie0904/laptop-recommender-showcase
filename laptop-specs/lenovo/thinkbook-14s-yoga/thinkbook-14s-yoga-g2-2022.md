# Lenovo ThinkBook 14s Yoga G2 (2022)

The ThinkBook 14s Yoga G2 is Lenovo's 2022 14-inch small-business convertible, built on 12th Generation Intel Core i5 / i7 Processor. It targets SMB users who want a pen-capable 360-degree convertible at mid-range prices.
