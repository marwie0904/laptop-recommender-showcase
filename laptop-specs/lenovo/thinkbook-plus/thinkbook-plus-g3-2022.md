# Lenovo ThinkBook Plus G3 (2022)

The ThinkBook Plus G3 is Lenovo's 2022 experimental-form-factor ThinkBook, built on 12th Generation Intel Core i5 / i7 Processor. It targets early adopters who want a second screen or unusual display in a business laptop.

## Best for
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (IPS, 99% DCI-P3).
- **Coding**: up to 32 GB RAM (soldered, choose capacity at purchase); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.
- **Dealbreakers**: RAM is soldered; buy the capacity you need upfront; heavy at 2.0 kg+; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
