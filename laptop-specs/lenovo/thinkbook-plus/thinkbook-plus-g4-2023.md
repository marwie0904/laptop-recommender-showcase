# Lenovo ThinkBook Plus G4 (2023)

The ThinkBook Plus G4 is Lenovo's 2023 experimental-form-factor ThinkBook, built on 13th Generation Intel U Series Core i5 / i7 Processor. It targets early adopters who want a second screen or unusual display in a business laptop.

## Best for
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (IPS, 100% DCI-P3).
- **Coding**: up to 16 GB RAM (soldered, choose capacity at purchase); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.
- **Business travel**: starts at 1.35 kg with up to 56 Wh battery.
- **Dealbreakers**: RAM is soldered; buy the capacity you need upfront; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
