## Best for
- **Coding**: up to 64 GB RAM (soldered on some configs, SO-DIMM on others); a full keyboard with numpad.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4, Ethernet for office docking and peripherals.
- **School**: entry business pricing (from ₱57,617 new in PH).
- **Dealbreakers**: on configs with soldered/on-package memory the RAM cannot be upgraded later; the base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
