## Best for
- **Coding**: up to 32 GB RAM (soldered, choose capacity at purchase); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.
- **Business travel**: starts at 1.25 kg with up to 46 Wh battery.
- **School**: durable fleet-class chassis.
- **Dealbreakers**: RAM is soldered; buy the capacity you need upfront; the base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
