# Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 6 (2023)

The ThinkPad P1 Gen 6 is Lenovo's 2023 thin 16-inch mobile workstation, built on 13th Generation H series Intel Core i7 / i9 processor, supports up to 14 cores, up to 5.4GHz. It targets engineers and creators who need ISV-certified discrete graphics in a slim chassis.
