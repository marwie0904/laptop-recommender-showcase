# Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 (2024)

The ThinkPad P1 Gen 7 is Lenovo's 2024 thin 16-inch mobile workstation, built on Intel H Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 / 9 Processor; supports up to 16 cores; up to 5.1GHz. It targets engineers and creators who need ISV-certified discrete graphics in a slim chassis.
