# Lenovo ThinkPad P16s Gen 3 (2024)

The ThinkPad P16s Gen 3 is Lenovo's 2024 entry 16-inch mobile workstation, built on Intel H Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 / 9 Processor; supports up to 16 cores; up to 5.1GHz. It targets engineers who want a large screen and ISV certification on a budget.

## Best for
- **Rendering / 3D**: discrete GPU options (NVIDIA RTX 500 Ada Generation Laptop GPU 4GB GDDR6) plus up to 96 GB RAM.
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (OLED, 100% sRGB).
- **Coding**: up to 96 GB RAM (SO-DIMM, upgradeable); a full keyboard with numpad.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4, Ethernet for office docking and peripherals.
- **Dealbreakers**: the base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available.
