## Best for
- **Rendering / 3D**: discrete GPU options (NVIDIA RTX 2000 Ada Generation Laptop GPU 8GB GDDR6, NVIDIA RTX A1000 Laptop GPU 6GB GDDR6, NVIDIA RTX A500 Laptop GPU 4GB GDDR6) plus up to 64 GB RAM.
- **Coding**: up to 64 GB RAM (SO-DIMM, upgradeable); a full keyboard with numpad.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.
- **Dealbreakers**: the base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available; heavy at 2.2 kg+.
