## Best for
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (OLED, 100% sRGB).
- **Coding**: up to 40 GB RAM (partly soldered + SO-DIMM); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4, Ethernet for office docking and peripherals.
- **Business travel**: starts at 1.32 kg with up to 52.5 Wh battery.
- **Dealbreakers**: part of the RAM is soldered with only one SO-DIMM slot, which can mean single-channel memory on base configs; the base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available.
