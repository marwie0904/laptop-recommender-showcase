## Best for
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (OLED, 100% DCI-P3, 100% sRGB).
- **Coding**: up to 64 GB RAM (SO-DIMM, upgradeable); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4, Ethernet for office docking and peripherals.
- **Business travel**: starts at 1.31 kg with up to 52.5 Wh battery.
- **Dealbreakers**: the base 300-nit 45% NTSC panel is dim and washed out; pick a 100% sRGB option if available; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
