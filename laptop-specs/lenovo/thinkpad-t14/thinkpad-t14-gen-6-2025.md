It targets office and enterprise users who want durability, serviceability and a good keyboard.

## Best for
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (OLED, 100% DCI-P3, 100% sRGB).
- **Coding**: up to 64 GB RAM (soldered on some configs, SO-DIMM on others); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4, Ethernet for office docking and peripherals.
- **Dealbreakers**: on configs with soldered/on-package memory the RAM cannot be upgraded later; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
