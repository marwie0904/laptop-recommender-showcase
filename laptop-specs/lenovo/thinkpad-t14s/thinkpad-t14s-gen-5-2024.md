# Lenovo ThinkPad T14s Gen 5 (2024)

The ThinkPad T14s Gen 5 is Lenovo's 2024 thin-and-light 14-inch business laptop, built on Intel U or H Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 Processor (Series 1). It targets professionals who want T-series features in a slimmer, lighter chassis.

- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.

- **Dealbreakers**: integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
