# Lenovo ThinkPad T14s Gen 6 (2024)

## Best for
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (OLED, 100% DCI-P3, 100% sRGB).
- **Coding**: up to 64 GB RAM (soldered, choose capacity at purchase); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.
- **Business travel**: starts at 1.24 kg with up to 58 Wh battery.
- **Dealbreakers**: integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering; Snapdragon (Arm) configs can hit app/driver compatibility gaps with legacy x86 software.
