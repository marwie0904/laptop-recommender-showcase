# Lenovo ThinkPad X1 2-in-1 Gen 10 Aura Edition (2025)

The ThinkPad X1 2-in-1 Gen 10 Aura Edition is Lenovo's 2025 premium 14-inch 360-degree convertible (successor to the X1 Yoga), built on Intel U or H Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 Processor (Series 2) - Arrow Lake (ARL) / Intel V Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 Processor (Series 2) - Lunar Lake (LNL). It targets executives who want an X1-class laptop that also works as a pen tablet.
