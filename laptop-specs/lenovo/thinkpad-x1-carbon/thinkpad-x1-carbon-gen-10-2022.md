## Best for
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (OLED, 100% sRGB).
- **Coding**: up to 32 GB RAM (soldered, choose capacity at purchase); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, HDMI, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.
- **Business travel**: starts at 1.12 kg with up to 57 Wh battery.
- **Dealbreakers**: RAM is soldered; buy the capacity you need upfront; integrated graphics only; not for serious gaming or GPU rendering.
