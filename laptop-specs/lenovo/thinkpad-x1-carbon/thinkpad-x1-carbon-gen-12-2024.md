# Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 12 (2024)

The ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 12 is Lenovo's 2024 flagship 14-inch carbon-fiber business ultrabook, built on Intel U or H Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 Processor (Series 1). It targets executives and frequent travelers who want the lightest full-size ThinkPad.
