# Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 Aura Edition (2025)

The ThinkPad X1 Carbon Gen 13 Aura Edition is Lenovo's 2025 flagship 14-inch carbon-fiber business ultrabook, built on Intel U or H Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 Processor (Series 2) - Arrow Lake (ARL) / Intel V Series Core Ultra 5 / 7 Processor (Series 2) - Lunar Lake (LNL). It targets executives and frequent travelers who want the lightest full-size ThinkPad.
