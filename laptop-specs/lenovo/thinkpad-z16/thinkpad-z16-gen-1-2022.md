## Best for
- **Rendering / 3D**: discrete GPU options (AMD Radeon RX 6500M 4GB GDDR6) plus up to 32 GB RAM.
- **Content creation**: color-accurate panel option (OLED, 100% DCI-P3, 100% sRGB).
- **Coding**: up to 32 GB RAM (soldered, choose capacity at purchase); a full keyboard.
- **Day-to-day**: USB-C charging, Thunderbolt/USB4 for office docking and peripherals.
- **Dealbreakers**: RAM is soldered; buy the capacity you need upfront.
