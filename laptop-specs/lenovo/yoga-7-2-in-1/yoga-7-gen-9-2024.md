# Lenovo Yoga 7i / Yoga 7 2-in-1 Gen 9 (2024)

## Best for
- School and note-taking: touch and pen support, up to 25.5 hr claimed video playback on the IPS 14IML9.
- Media and light editing: 2.8K 120 Hz OLED option at 100% DCI-P3 (Intel 14" only).
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM, integrated graphics only, 16" panel is 45% NTSC 60 Hz, NPU is below the Copilot+ threshold.
