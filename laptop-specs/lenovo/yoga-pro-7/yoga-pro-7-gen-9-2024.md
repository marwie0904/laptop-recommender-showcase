## Best for
- Creator work: 2.8K 120Hz OLED (100% P3) or 3K 120Hz IPS, optional RTX 4060 (60W) on 14IMH9.
- Local AI / Copilot+: 14ASP9 (50 TOPS NPU).
- Coding and day-to-day: 32 GB RAM options, 73 Wh battery, 14.5" 16:10.
- Dealbreakers: RAM soldered, discrete-GPU models drop to a single SSD slot, no card reader or fingerprint reader. 14AHP9 caps at 16 GB RAM.
