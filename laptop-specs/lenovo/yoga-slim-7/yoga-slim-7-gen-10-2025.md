## Best for
- Day-to-day and school: Lunar Lake efficiency, 70 Wh battery, 1.19 kg.
- Business travel: aluminium body, Thunderbolt 4, Wi-Fi 7, e-shutter camera.
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM (pick 32 GB if keeping it long), no fingerprint reader, only 8 CPU cores for heavy multi-thread work.
