# Lenovo Yoga Slim 7i Gen 9 (2024)

Aluminium ultrabook in two versions: 14" Meteor Lake H (Core Ultra Series 1) with OLED, and 15.3" Lunar Lake (Core Ultra 7 200V, Aura Edition) with IPS or OLED. PSREF machines covered: Yoga Slim 7 14IMH9 (page dated 13 Jan 2026) and Yoga Slim 7 15ILL9 (4 Sep 2026). Only the 14IMH9 was seen in PH retail.
