## Best for
- Day-to-day office and school work: all-day claimed battery (17-18 h), 3:2 display.
- Dealbreakers: only one USB-C port, 720p webcam, Wi-Fi 6, RAM soldered. Newer Snapdragon and Core Ultra models have better battery and two USB-C ports.
