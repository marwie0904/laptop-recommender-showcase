# Microsoft Surface Laptop 8th Edition (2026)

Successor to the Surface Laptop 7th Edition in 13.8" and 15". Consumer models use Snapdragon X2 (available 2026-06-16). Surface Laptop for Business 8th Edition comes in two forms: Snapdragon X2 and Intel Core Ultra Series 3 (Panther Lake). Business models ship from 2026-07-14.

## Best for
- Office work and travel: 19-23 h claimed battery, 120 Hz display, roughly 1.3 kg (13.8").
- Pick the Intel business version when x86 app compatibility matters. Snapdragon X2 emulates x86 apps.
- Dealbreakers: not in PH retail yet, high US pricing.
