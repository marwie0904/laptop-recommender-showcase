## Pricing (Philippines)
- New: ₱89,999 - ₱156,500 (Villman October 2026 for Snapdragon, PCWorx for Intel, Intel units out of stock). Many Villman units include a free keyboard and Slim Pen 2.
- Used: No reliable PH used-market data found. One Carousell snippet showed a used Pro 11 (X Plus, 16 GB/256 GB) at ₱37,500, but the page could not be opened to verify.

## Best for
- Note-taking, pen use, travel: 895 g, 120 Hz OLED option, ~14 h video playback.
- Pick the Intel business version for x86 compatibility. Dealbreakers: Snapdragon emulation, keyboard extra.
