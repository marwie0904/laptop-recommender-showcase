# Microsoft Surface Pro 12th Edition (13-inch, 2026)

13" detachable 2-in-1. Consumer model uses Snapdragon X2 Plus/Elite (on sale 2026-06-16). Surface Pro for Business 12th Edition adds an Intel Core Ultra Series 3 (Panther Lake) variant (unveiled 2026-05-19) and Snapdragon X2 versions (from 2026-07-14). Keyboard (Pro Flex Keyboard) and pen sold separately. PH retailers label it "Surface Pro 12".
