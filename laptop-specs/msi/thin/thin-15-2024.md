## Best for
- School and day-to-day: cheapest RTX laptop MSI sells in PH, upgradeable RAM and 2.5" bay.
- Esports and light 1080p gaming: all GPUs capped at 45 W, so a 45 W RTX 4050 performs well below 100 W+ versions.
- Dealbreakers: 8 GB single-channel base configs, 45% NTSC washed-out panel, 52 Wh battery, DDR4.
