- PH pricing: only BenStore PC (Manila Razer reseller) lists Razer laptops in Oct 2026, and only current models (Blade 14 2025, Blade 16 2025/2026, Blade 18 2026). Blade 18 2025 PH prices come from earlier BenStore listings seen in search results. PC Express, DataBlitz and LazMall/Shopee Mall had no Razer laptop listings. For 2022–2024 models no authorized PH price was found. Those files keep `ph_price_new_php: null` and give US/EU launch prices in the body. `usd_msrp` is filled only for Blade 14 2022/2023, where both ends of the US range were verified.
- PH used prices: Carousell PH has very few Razer Blade 2022+ listings. Every file says "No reliable PH used-market data found".
