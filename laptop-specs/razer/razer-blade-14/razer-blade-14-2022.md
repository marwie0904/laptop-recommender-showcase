## Best for
- Portable gaming: RTX 3070 Ti/3080 Ti at 100 W in a 1.78 kg 14-inch chassis handles 1440p gaming.
- Coding and day-to-day: 8-core CPU, solid keyboard, plenty of USB ports.
- Dealbreakers: 16 GB soldered RAM ceiling, keyboard deck gets very hot under load, 61.6 Wh battery gives short unplugged runtime.
