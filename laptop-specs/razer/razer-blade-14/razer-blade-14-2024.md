Minor refresh of the 2023 Blade 14: Ryzen 9 8945HS "Hawk Point" with NPU, 140 W RTX 4060/4070, Wi-Fi 7 and a factory-calibrated 240 Hz QHD+ panel in the same aluminum chassis.

## Best for
- Portable 1440p gaming: RTX 4070 holds ~114 W in games, slightly faster than the 2023 model.
- Color work on the go: factory-calibrated 100% DCI-P3 panel (deltaE ~2.2).
- Coding and multitasking: RAM upgradeable to 64 GB.
- Dealbreakers: tops out at RTX 4070 with 8 GB VRAM, warm surfaces even at idle, single SSD slot.
