# Razer Blade 15 (2022)

Thin 15.6-inch aluminum gaming laptop with 12th-gen Intel Alder Lake-H and RTX 30 Ti GPUs at 110 W, offered with fast IPS or (from late 2022) a QHD 240 Hz OLED panel.

## Best for
- Gaming and creative work: 240 Hz QHD with full DCI-P3 (IPS) or OLED.
- Long-term ownership: two SODIMM slots, two M.2 slots and replaceable Wi-Fi.
- Dealbreakers: GPUs capped at 110 W (rivals run 150 W+ versions of the same chip), warm keyboard (~48 C gaming), short battery life vs rivals, glossy OLED flickers at low brightness.
