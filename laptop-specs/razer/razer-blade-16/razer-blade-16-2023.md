# Razer Blade 16 (2023)

First Blade 16: Core i9-13950HX with RTX 4060 to 4090 at up to 175 W, and the first dual-mode mini-LED panel (UHD+ 120 Hz / FHD+ 240 Hz). Aimed at gamers and creators who want desktop-class power in a 16-inch aluminum chassis.

## Best for
- High-end gaming: RTX 4080/4090 at 175 W (157–162 W sustained in games).
- Content creation and rendering: 24-core i9-13950HX, 100% DCI-P3, optional 4K mini-LED, up to 64 GB RAM and two SSD slots.
- Dealbreakers: heavy at 2.45 kg, very expensive, no Ethernet, early mini-LED units reported image retention.
