## Best for
- Thin high-end gaming: RTX 5090 in a 2.1 kg, 17.4 mm chassis, slightly faster in games than the 2025 model.
- Business travel / productivity: up to 13 h battery claim, Thunderbolt 5 docking, IR login.
- Content creation: 1,100-nit HDR OLED with Calman verification.
- Dealbreakers: soldered RAM, CPU multi-thread ~10–15% slower than the 2025 Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, priced higher than the 2025 model and most rivals.
