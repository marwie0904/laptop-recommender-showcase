# Razer Blade 17 (2022)

Last generation of Razer's 17.3-inch Blade: 12th-gen Intel Alder Lake-H with RTX 30 Ti GPUs at up to 165 W, in the same aluminum chassis as 2021. Replaced by the Blade 18 in 2023.

## Best for
- Desktop-replacement gaming: 165 W RTX 3080 Ti runs well above the ~100 W versions in thinner laptops.
- Content creation and rendering: 100% DCI-P3 QHD or 4K panel, upgradeable RAM, two SSD slots, SD reader, 2.5 GbE.
- Dealbreakers: heavy (2.7+ kg), CPU sustains only ~60 W under long loads, discontinued with no PH retail stock.
