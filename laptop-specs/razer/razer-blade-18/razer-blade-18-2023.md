# Razer Blade 18 (2023)

Razer's first 18-inch Blade and replacement for the Blade 17: Raptor Lake-HX Core i9 with RTX 4060 to 4090, a 240 Hz QHD+ panel, 2.5 GbE and full upgradeability. A desktop-replacement for gamers and creators.

## Best for
- Desktop-replacement gaming: RTX 4080/4090 at 175 W, large 240 Hz QHD+ panel.
- Rendering and content creation: 24-core HX CPU, 100% DCI-P3, two SODIMM and two M.2 slots, 2.5 GbE.
- Dealbreakers: 3 kg plus large charger, RTX 4070 config underperformed in Notebookcheck's tests (~80 W in Witcher 3, frame drops), only one Thunderbolt port.
