## Best for
- Desktop-replacement gaming: 175 W RTX 4090 and a 300 Hz mini-LED that reaches 1,200+ nits in HDR.
- Rendering / creator docks: Thunderbolt 5, 2.5 GbE, two SSD slots, upgradeable RAM.
- Dealbreakers: 3.1 kg, local dimming off by default, RTX 4070 config ~35–40% slower than RTX 4090, hot GaN adapter.
