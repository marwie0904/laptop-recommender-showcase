# Samsung Galaxy Book2 Go (2023)

Fanless 14" Windows on Arm laptop with Snapdragon 7c+ Gen 3, optional 5G. Launched January 2023 (previewed at CES 2023), so it is not a 2022 launch, but it is sold as "Galaxy Book2". Targets budget buyers who want long battery life and web/Office use.

- School and web/Office use: fanless, silent, long battery.
- Travel with 5G: optional cellular model.
- Dealbreakers: 4 GB RAM soldered, low-power Arm chip, Intel-app emulation limits, no backlit keyboard.
