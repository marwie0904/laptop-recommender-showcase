## Best for
- School and note-taking: S Pen is in the box, AMOLED touch panel.
- Business travel: 1.04 kg (13.3"), 65 W USB-C charging, up to 21 h claimed.
- Day-to-day and light creative work: sharp AMOLED color. Iris Xe only, no discrete GPU.
- Dealbreakers: RAM is soldered. Only 60 Hz. Price in PH is above ₱100k.
