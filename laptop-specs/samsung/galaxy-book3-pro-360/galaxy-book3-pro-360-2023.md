# Samsung Galaxy Book3 Pro 360 (2023)

16" convertible with a touch 3K 120 Hz AMOLED display and bundled S Pen, on 13th-gen Intel P-series chips. Targets users who want a large pen-capable 2-in-1. Only a 16" size was found (no 14" Pro 360 for 2023). US launch February 17, 2023.
