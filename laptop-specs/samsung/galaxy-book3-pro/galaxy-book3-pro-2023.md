# Samsung Galaxy Book3 Pro (2023)

Thin non-touch clamshell with a 3K 120 Hz AMOLED screen and 13th-gen Intel P-series chips, in 14" and 16" sizes. Targets professionals who want a light OLED ultrabook. US launch February 17, 2023.
