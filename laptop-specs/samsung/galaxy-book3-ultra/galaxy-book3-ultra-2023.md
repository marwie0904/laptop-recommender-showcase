# Samsung Galaxy Book3 Ultra (2023)

16" creator laptop with a 3K 120 Hz AMOLED display, Core i7-H / i9-H chips and GeForce RTX 4050 or RTX 4070 laptop graphics. Samsung's first Book with a discrete GPU of this class. US launch February 24, 2023.
