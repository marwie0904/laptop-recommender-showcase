# Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge (2024)

Windows on Arm Copilot+ PC with Snapdragon X Elite, 3K 120 Hz touch AMOLED display, in 14" and 16" sizes. Targets travelers and office users who want battery life and a thin chassis, and who run Arm-compatible apps. The separate 15.6" Edge is in its own file.
