# Samsung Galaxy Book4 Pro (2024)

Thin touch-AMOLED clamshell with Intel Core Ultra (Meteor Lake) H-series chips and integrated Arc graphics, in 14" and 16" sizes. Targets professionals who want a light OLED laptop with an NPU. Global launch February 26, 2024 (Korea earlier).
