# Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro 360 (2024)

First Galaxy Book5 model: a 16-inch AMOLED convertible with Intel Lunar Lake (Core Ultra Series 2), in-box S Pen and Copilot+ PC support. Targets students and creators who want a large pen-enabled OLED with long battery life.

## Best for
- Note-taking and drawing: 16-inch touch AMOLED with in-box S Pen.
- Day-to-day and school: Lunar Lake efficiency, up to 25 h video playback claim, light 1.69 kg for a 16-inch.
- Media: excellent AMOLED and quad Dolby Atmos speakers.
- Dealbreakers: RAM not upgradeable, high price, low keyboard travel, multi-core performance barely above the previous Core Ultra 5 125H.
