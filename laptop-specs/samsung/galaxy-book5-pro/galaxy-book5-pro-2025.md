# Samsung Galaxy Book5 Pro (2025)

Thin AMOLED clamshell in 14- and 16-inch sizes with Intel Lunar Lake (Core Ultra Series 2), a 120 Hz touch display and long battery life. Samsung's MacBook Air rival for travelers and students.

## Best for
- Business travel and school: 1.23 kg 14-inch, Lunar Lake efficiency, Thunderbolt 4 and HDMI 2.1.
- Day-to-day and media: bright 120 Hz AMOLED and quad Dolby Atmos speakers.
- Coding: 32 GB option and a second SSD slot on the 14".
- Dealbreakers: RAM not upgradeable, loses to MacBook Air M4 in performance and battery life (Notebookcheck), glossy screen.
