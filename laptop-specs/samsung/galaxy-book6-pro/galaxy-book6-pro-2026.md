# Samsung Galaxy Book6 Pro (2026)

Thin aluminum AMOLED clamshell in 14- and 16-inch sizes with Intel Core Ultra Series 3 (Panther Lake), a 1,000-nit HDR touch display and long battery life. Successor to the Book5 Pro for travelers and professionals.
