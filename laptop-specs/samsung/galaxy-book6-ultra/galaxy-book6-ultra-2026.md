# Samsung Galaxy Book6 Ultra (2026)

Samsung's flagship 16-inch creator laptop: Intel Core Ultra Series 3 (Panther Lake), optional RTX 5060/5070, a 1,000-nit anti-reflective AMOLED touch display and an 80 Wh battery in a 15.4 mm aluminum chassis.

## Best for
- Content creation and photo/video editing: 1,000-nit HDR anti-reflective AMOLED, RTX GPU, second SSD slot.
- Rendering and light gaming: RTX 5070 at 90 W handles 1080p/1440p games and GPU-accelerated apps.
- Business travel: up to 30 h video playback claim, 1.8–1.9 kg, USB-C charging.
- Dealbreakers: RTX limited to 90 W and 8 GB VRAM, soldered RAM, aluminum shows fingerprints.
